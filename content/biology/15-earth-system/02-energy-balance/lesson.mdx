<ConceptCard title="Uneven heating" emoji="☀️">
Near the equator, sunlight strikes almost head-on and is concentrated. Near the poles it arrives at a slant and spreads over a larger area. This uneven heating drives winds and ocean currents that move heat towards the poles.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="Enhanced greenhouse effect" emoji="🏭">
Burning fossil fuels and cutting forests have raised CO₂ from about 280 to over 420 ppm, trapping extra heat. Earth has already warmed by about 1.2 °C since the 1800s.
</ConceptCard>
