<ConceptCard title="Humans and the cycles" emoji="🏭">
Burning fossil fuels moves carbon that was locked away for millions of years into the air in decades. Factory-made fertilisers add huge amounts of nitrogen to fields; excess runs into rivers and causes algal blooms. Protecting forests, wetlands and soils helps keep the cycles in balance.
</ConceptCard>
