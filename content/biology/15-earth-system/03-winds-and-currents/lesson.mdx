</Hook>

<ConceptCard title="Ocean currents" emoji="🌊">
Winds and differences in water temperature and saltiness drive huge **ocean currents**. Warm currents carry heat from the tropics towards the poles; cold currents return. They shape the climate of coasts around the world.
</ConceptCard>
