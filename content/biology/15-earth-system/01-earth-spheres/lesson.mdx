<ConceptCard title="A system" emoji="🔗">
A <KeyTerm def="A set of parts that interact and exchange energy and matter, so a change in one part affects the others.">system</KeyTerm> is a set of connected parts. The Sun powers the Earth system; matter such as water and carbon moves between spheres. Because of these links, a change in one sphere (like cutting a forest) spreads to others (soil erosion, less rain, more CO₂).
</ConceptCard>
