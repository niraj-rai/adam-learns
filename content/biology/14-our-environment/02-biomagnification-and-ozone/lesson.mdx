<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="Biological magnification" emoji="📈">
Some chemicals, like DDT, are <KeyTerm def="Not broken down by microbes or by the body.">non-biodegradable</KeyTerm> and dissolve in fat. They are not excreted, so they build up in body tissue. Each animal eats many from the level below, so the concentration rises at each step. Humans, at the top of many food chains, are exposed too.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="The ozone shield" emoji="🛡️">
<KeyTerm def="A molecule of three oxygen atoms (O₃). High in the atmosphere it absorbs harmful UV radiation.">Ozone</KeyTerm> forms high in the atmosphere when UV splits O₂ and the atoms join other O₂ molecules. It absorbs UV rays that cause skin cancer and cataracts. **CFCs** from fridges and aerosols broke down ozone, creating an “ozone hole” over Antarctica. The **Montreal Protocol** (1987) phased them out, and the ozone layer is expected to recover by about the 2060s.
</ConceptCard>
