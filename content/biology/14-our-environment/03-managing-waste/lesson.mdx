<ConceptCard title="The 3 Rs" emoji="♻️">
**Reduce** what you use (the most effective), **reuse** what you can, and **recycle** the rest. Separating wet and dry waste at home makes composting and recycling possible. Kulhads, leaf plates and steel tiffins are traditional Indian examples of low-waste living.
</ConceptCard>
