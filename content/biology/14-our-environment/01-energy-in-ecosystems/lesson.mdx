<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="Trophic levels" emoji="🔺">
**1st**: producers (plants, algae). **2nd**: herbivores (primary consumers). **3rd**: carnivores (secondary consumers). **4th**: top carnivores. <KeyTerm def="Organisms, such as bacteria and fungi, that break down dead matter and return nutrients to the soil.">Decomposers</KeyTerm> feed on dead matter at every level.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="The 10% law" emoji="📉">
Green plants capture only about 1% of the sunlight falling on their leaves. Then only about **10%** of the energy at one level reaches the next; the rest is used in respiration and lost as heat. So energy flow is **one-way**: it cannot return to the Sun. Nutrients, though, are recycled by decomposers.
</ConceptCard>
