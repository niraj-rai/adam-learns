<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="Genes and alleles" emoji="🧬">
A <KeyTerm def="A section of DNA that controls a characteristic.">gene</KeyTerm> can come in different versions, called <KeyTerm def="Different forms of the same gene, such as T (tall) and t (dwarf).">alleles</KeyTerm>. You get one allele from each parent. A **dominant** allele (T) shows even with one copy; a **recessive** allele (t) shows only in tt. Your alleles are your **genotype**; how you look is your **phenotype**.
</ConceptCard>

<StepReveal
  steps={[

    "F₂ genotypes: 1 TT : 2 Tt : 1 tt.",
    "F₂ phenotypes: 3 tall : 1 dwarf. The dwarf trait reappears!"

<ConceptCard title="Two traits at once" emoji="🟡">
Crossing round yellow (RRYY) with wrinkled green (rryy) peas and self-crossing the F₁ gives **9 : 3 : 3 : 1**. The traits are inherited independently.
</ConceptCard>
