<ConceptCard title="Human endocrine glands" emoji="🧬">
**Pituitary**: growth hormone (the master gland). **Thyroid**: thyroxine (needs iodine; lack causes goitre). **Pancreas**: insulin (lowers blood sugar). **Adrenal**: adrenaline (fight or flight). **Testes/ovaries**: testosterone, oestrogen. Hormone levels are kept steady by <KeyTerm def="When the effect of a hormone signals the gland to make less or more of it.">feedback</KeyTerm>: when blood sugar rises, the pancreas releases insulin; when it falls, insulin release drops.
</ConceptCard>
