<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="XX and XY" emoji="🧬">
Humans have 23 pairs of chromosomes. One pair are the **sex chromosomes**: females are **XX**, males **XY**. Every egg carries an X; half the sperm carry X and half carry Y. So X-sperm + egg → XX (girl); Y-sperm + egg → XY (boy).
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="Not the same everywhere" emoji="🐢">
In some reptiles, the temperature at which the eggs are incubated decides the sex. In snails, individuals can change sex.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="Where variation comes from" emoji="🎲">
Sexual reproduction mixes alleles from two parents, so every child (except identical twins) is a new combination. Small copying errors in DNA add more variation. Variation helps a species survive changing conditions.
</ConceptCard>
