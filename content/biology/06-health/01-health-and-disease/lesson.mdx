<Stage kind="explain" title="Health and disease" />

<ConceptCard title="Health" emoji="💚">
The World Health Organization describes health as a state of complete **physical, mental and social** wellbeing, not merely the absence of disease. Clean water, good food, sanitation, rest, exercise, friendships and access to healthcare all affect health.
</ConceptCard>

<Stage kind="try" title="How Does It Spread?" />

<Lab id="disease-sorter" />

<ConceptCard title="Routes of spread" emoji="🛣️">
- **Air**: droplets from coughs and sneezes (cold, flu, TB, measles).
- **Water and food**: contaminated by faeces (cholera, typhoid, hepatitis A).
- **Contact**: touching an infected person or object (ringworm, some skin infections).
- **Vectors**: animals that carry pathogens, especially mosquitoes (malaria, dengue, chikungunya).
</ConceptCard>
