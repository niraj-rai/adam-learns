<Stage kind="explain" title="Breaking the chain" />

<ConceptCard title="The chain of infection" emoji="⛓️">
A germ needs a **source** (an infected person), a **route** (air, hands, water, a mosquito) and a new **host**. Break any link and the chain stops:
- **Handwashing** with soap removes germs from hands.
- **Covering coughs** and masks when ill block droplets.
- **Ventilation** dilutes germs in the air.
- **Staying home** when ill removes the source.
- **Safe water and toilets** block water-borne germs.
</ConceptCard>
