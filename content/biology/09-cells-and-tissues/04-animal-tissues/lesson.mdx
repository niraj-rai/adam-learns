<MythFact myth="Bones are dead and never change.">
Bone is living tissue with blood vessels and cells that constantly break down old bone and build new bone. That's why broken bones heal, and why exercise and calcium make bones stronger.
</MythFact>

<DidYouKnow>
The longest cells in your body are neurons: a single nerve cell can stretch from the bottom of your spine to your big toe, about 1 m long.
</DidYouKnow>
