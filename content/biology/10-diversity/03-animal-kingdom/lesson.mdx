<ConceptCard title="Cold- and warm-blooded" emoji="🌡️">
Fish, amphibians and reptiles are **cold-blooded**: their body temperature changes with their surroundings. Birds and mammals are **warm-blooded**: they keep a steady body temperature, so they can stay active in cold weather.
</ConceptCard>
