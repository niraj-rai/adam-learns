<Hook emoji="🌿">
Green scum in a pond, moss on a monsoon wall, a feathery fern, a Himalayan deodar and a mango tree are all plants. But only one of them ever flowers. Botanists sort plants by how complex their bodies are and how they reproduce.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="A plant has roots, stems, leaves and water-carrying tissue, but reproduces by spores. What is it?"

Ferns (Pteridophyta) have vascular tissue but **no seeds**. Mosses have no vascular tissue; pines make seeds.

<ConceptCard title="Monocots and dicots" emoji="🌾">
Angiosperms are split by the number of seed leaves (<KeyTerm def="The first leaves in a seed, which store or absorb food for the young plant. Monocots have one; dicots have two.">cotyledons</KeyTerm>):
- **Monocots**: one cotyledon, parallel leaf veins, fibrous roots, e.g. wheat, rice, maize, banana.
- **Dicots**: two cotyledons, net-like veins, a tap root, e.g. mango, pea, neem.
</ConceptCard>

<RealLife>
