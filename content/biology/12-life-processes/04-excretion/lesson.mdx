<Hook emoji="🫘">
Every day your two fist-sized kidneys filter around 180 litres of fluid from your blood, yet you pass only about 1.5 litres of urine. Nearly everything useful is taken back.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst

Useful substances like glucose, amino acids and most water are **selectively reabsorbed**.

<ConceptCard title="When kidneys fail" emoji="🩺">
In <KeyTerm def="Using a machine to filter wastes from the blood when the kidneys fail.">dialysis</KeyTerm>, blood flows through tubes bathed in fluid. Urea diffuses out, but the fluid contains glucose and salts so these stay in. A kidney transplant is the long-term alternative.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="Excretion in plants" emoji="🍂">
Plants release oxygen and water vapour through stomata, store some wastes in old leaves that fall, or in bark, and some as resins and gums.
</ConceptCard>
