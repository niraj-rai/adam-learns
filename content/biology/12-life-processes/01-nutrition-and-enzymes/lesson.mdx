<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="Two ways to get food" emoji="🌿">
<KeyTerm def="An organism that makes its own food from simple substances, such as plants using photosynthesis.">Autotrophs</KeyTerm> make food by photosynthesis: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ (light, chlorophyll). <KeyTerm def="An organism that takes in food made by others.">Heterotrophs</KeyTerm> eat other organisms, and must digest the food first.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="How enzymes work" emoji="🔑">
An <KeyTerm def="A protein that speeds up a reaction in living things without being used up: a biological catalyst.">enzyme</KeyTerm> has an **active site** that fits its substrate like a lock and key. Each enzyme has an **optimum** temperature (about 37 °C in humans) and pH (pepsin ≈ 2, amylase ≈ 7). Too hot, and the active site changes shape: the enzyme is <KeyTerm def="Permanently changed in shape so it no longer works.">denatured</KeyTerm>.
</ConceptCard>
