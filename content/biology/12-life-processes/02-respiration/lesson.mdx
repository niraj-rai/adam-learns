<ConceptCard title="ATP, the energy currency" emoji="⚡">
The energy released is stored in <KeyTerm def="Adenosine triphosphate: a molecule that carries energy to where the cell needs it.">ATP</KeyTerm>, which powers muscle contraction, making proteins and nerve impulses.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="Gas exchange" emoji="🫁">
In the lungs, air passes down the trachea and bronchi to millions of <KeyTerm def="Tiny air sacs in the lungs where oxygen and carbon dioxide are exchanged.">alveoli</KeyTerm>: thin, moist and surrounded by capillaries. Haemoglobin in red blood cells carries oxygen. Plants exchange gases through stomata and lenticels; they respire all the time.
</ConceptCard>
