<Stage kind="explain" title="One parent" />

<ConceptCard title="Asexual reproduction" emoji="🧬">
In <KeyTerm def="Reproduction involving only one parent, producing offspring identical to it.">asexual reproduction</KeyTerm>, one parent produces offspring identical to itself. In plants, new plants can grow from stems, leaves, roots, tubers and bulbs: **vegetative propagation**.
</ConceptCard>

<CbseCorner source="Earlier NCERT Class 7 Ch 12: Reproduction in Plants">
- **Asexual reproduction**: vegetative propagation (potato, ginger, rose, Bryophyllum), budding (yeast), fragmentation (Spirogyra), spore formation (bread mould, ferns).
- Plants produced by vegetative propagation are identical to the parent.
