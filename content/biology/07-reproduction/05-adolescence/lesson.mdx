<Stage kind="explain" title="Adolescence and puberty" />

<ConceptCard title="What changes?" emoji="📋">
<KeyTerm def="The period of life, roughly between 10 and 19 years, when a child grows into an adult.">Adolescence</KeyTerm> includes **puberty**, when the body becomes capable of reproduction. Common changes:
- A **growth spurt**: getting taller quickly.
- More active **sweat and oil glands**, sometimes causing pimples.
- Growth of body hair.
- In boys, the voice box grows and the voice deepens (the "Adam's apple").
- In girls, the body shape changes and **menstruation** (periods) begins: a natural, healthy part of growing up.
- **Emotional changes**: new feelings, more independence, and sometimes mood swings.
</ConceptCard>

<Stage kind="try" title="Growing Up" />

<Lab id="growing-up" />

<ConceptCard title="Hormones: the messengers" emoji="🧪">
**Hormones** are chemicals made by **endocrine glands** and carried in the blood. The **pituitary gland** in the brain signals the testes (in boys) and ovaries (in girls) to make hormones such as testosterone and oestrogen, which bring about the changes of puberty.
</ConceptCard>
