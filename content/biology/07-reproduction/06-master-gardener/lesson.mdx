The principal has put you in charge of the school garden for a whole year. You want more potatoes, prize roses, bumper pumpkins, happy bees and new trees spreading across the hillside. Every problem you meet can be solved with what you know about how plants reproduce.
</Hook>

<ConceptCard title="Your toolkit" emoji="🧰">
- **Vegetative propagation** makes identical copies quickly: tubers, cuttings, leaves, bulbs.
- **Pollination** moves pollen from anther to stigma; no pollination means no seeds or fruit.
- **Pollinators** need nectar-rich flowers and freedom from harmful pesticides.
- **Seed dispersal** spreads seeds away to avoid competition.
</ConceptCard>
