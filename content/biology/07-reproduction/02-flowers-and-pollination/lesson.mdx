<Lab id="flower-lab" />

<StepReveal>
<div>
**1. Pollination**: pollen moves from an anther to a stigma, by insects, wind, birds or water.
</div>
<div>
**2. Fertilisation**: a male cell from the pollen reaches an ovule and fuses with the egg cell, forming a **zygote**.
</div>
<div>
**3. Seed and fruit**: the zygote grows into an **embryo** inside the ovule, which becomes a **seed**. The **ovary** becomes the **fruit**, protecting the seeds.
</div>
</StepReveal>

<CbseCorner source="Earlier NCERT Class 7 Ch 12">
- Parts of a flower: **stamen** (anther, filament) and **pistil** (stigma, style, ovary).
- **Pollination**: self and cross; agents are wind, water and insects.
- **Fertilisation** forms a zygote; the ovary becomes the fruit and the ovules become seeds.
</CbseCorner>
