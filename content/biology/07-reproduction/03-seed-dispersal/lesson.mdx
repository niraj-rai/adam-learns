Drumstick seeds have paper-thin wings. Madar seeds float away on silky parachutes. Coconuts can drift across oceans to new islands. Burrs hitch rides on your socks and a dog's fur. Seeds can't walk, so how and why do they travel so far?

Seedlings under the parent get little light and share water and nutrients with a big tree. **Dispersal** gives them space and lets the plant spread to new places.
</PredictFirst>
