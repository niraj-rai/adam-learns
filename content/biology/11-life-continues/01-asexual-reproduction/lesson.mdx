<Hook emoji="🦠">
A single bacterium in warm food can divide every 20 minutes. After just 8 hours that one cell could have become more than **16 million**. No partner, no seeds, no eggs: just copying. This is asexual reproduction.
</Hook>

<Lab id="clone-lab" />

<ConceptCard title="Clones: pros and cons" emoji="⚖️">
Offspring of asexual reproduction are <KeyTerm def="Genetically identical copies of a single parent.">clones</KeyTerm>.
- ✅ Fast; only one parent needed; good plants (like a sweet banana variety) can be copied exactly.
- ❌ No variation, so a single disease or change in climate can wipe out a whole population.
</ConceptCard>

<RealLife>
Almost all the bananas you eat are clones grown from suckers, which is why a single fungal disease (Panama disease) can threaten banana farms worldwide. Farmers and scientists in India use tissue culture to produce thousands of disease-free clones of bananas, sugarcane and orchids.
</RealLife>
