<Hook emoji="🐝">
Mango farmers in Andhra Pradesh and Maharashtra know that when there are fewer bees and flies, their trees produce fewer mangoes. Fruits and seeds only form after two events inside a flower: **pollination** and **fertilisation**.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="After fertilisation, which part of the flower becomes the seed?"
  options={["The ovary", "The petal", "The ovule", "The anther"]}
  answer={2}
>
The **ovule** (containing the fertilised egg) becomes the **seed**; the **ovary** around it becomes the **fruit**.
</PredictFirst>

<MythFact myth="Pollination is the same thing as fertilisation.">
Pollination just delivers pollen to the stigma. **Fertilisation** is the fusion of the male and female gametes inside the ovule, and it happens only after the pollen tube has grown down to it.
</MythFact>
