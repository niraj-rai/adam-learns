<ConceptCard title="From fertilisation to birth" emoji="🤰">
- A sperm fuses with an egg in the oviduct → **zygote**.
- The zygote divides and **implants** in the thick lining of the uterus.
- The <KeyTerm def="An organ that forms in the uterus during pregnancy. It passes oxygen and food from the mother's blood to the baby and wastes back, without the two bloods mixing.">placenta</KeyTerm> forms, linked to the baby by the umbilical cord.
- After about 9 months the uterus muscles contract and the baby is born.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="The menstrual cycle" emoji="🗓️">
From puberty, about once a month an ovary releases an egg and the uterus lining thickens, ready for a fertilised egg. If fertilisation doesn't happen, the lining breaks down and leaves the body as a period (menstruation), usually for a few days. The cycle is controlled by hormones and is a normal, healthy part of growing up.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="Reproductive health" emoji="🩺">
Good reproductive health means a healthy body, good hygiene (including menstrual hygiene), accurate information and respect. It includes knowing that some infections can be passed on through sexual contact, and that contraception lets people plan families. India's law sets 18 as the minimum age of marriage for women and 21 for men, partly to protect young people's health and education.
</ConceptCard>
