<Hook emoji="👩‍🍳">
A good South Indian kitchen is a microbiology lab in disguise. The cook encourages friendly microbes to ferment batter and set curd, and keeps harmful ones away from the leftovers. Today, you're the chef, and the orders are coming in!
</Hook>

<ConceptCard title="Your toolkit" emoji="🧰">
- Fermenting microbes need **warmth** (roughly 30–40 °C), food and time.
- **Too cold**: they're slow. **Too hot** (above about 55 °C): they die.
- **Curd**: lukewarm milk + a spoonful of starter.
- **Leftovers**: cool quickly and **refrigerate**; reheat until boiling before eating.
</ConceptCard>
