<PredictFirst

  options={["It kills all microbes", "It slows microbes down", "It removes air", "It adds salt"]}
  answer={1}

Cold **slows** microbes' growth but doesn't kill them. That's why food still spoils eventually, even in the fridge.
</PredictFirst>

<Stage kind="explain" title="Spoilage and preservation" />

<ConceptCard title="Spoilage" emoji="🤢">
Microbes in food feed and multiply, producing smells, slime, mould and sometimes **toxins**. Eating spoiled food can cause **food poisoning**. Some spoiled food looks and smells fine but is still unsafe.
</ConceptCard>
