<Stage kind="explain" title="Fermentation" />

<ConceptCard title="What is fermentation?" emoji="🫧">
<KeyTerm def="A process in which microorganisms break down sugars without much oxygen, producing substances like carbon dioxide, alcohol or lactic acid.">Fermentation</KeyTerm> is when microbes break down sugars to get energy, releasing useful products:
- **Yeast** → carbon dioxide (bread rises) and a little alcohol.
- **Lactobacillus** → lactic acid (milk turns into curd, with its sour taste).
- In **idli and dosa batter**, lactic acid bacteria and yeasts work together, making the batter rise and taste slightly sour.
</ConceptCard>

<Lab id="fermentation-lab" />

<ConceptCard title="What microbes need" emoji="🌡️">
- **Food** (sugars), **water** and **warmth**: about 35–40 °C is ideal.
- **Too cold**: they work very slowly. **Too hot** (above about 55 °C): they die.
- A **starter** (a spoonful of old curd) provides the microbes to begin with.
</ConceptCard>
