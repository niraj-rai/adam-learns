<Stage kind="explain" title="Decomposers" />

<ConceptCard title="Recycling nutrients" emoji="🍂">
<KeyTerm def="Organisms, mainly bacteria and fungi, that break down dead plants and animals, returning nutrients to the soil.">Decomposers</KeyTerm> such as bacteria and fungi feed on dead plants and animals, breaking them into simple substances that return to the soil. New plants use these nutrients to grow. Without decomposers, dead matter would pile up and nutrients would be locked away forever.
</ConceptCard>

<Stage kind="try" title="Compost bin and root nodules" />

<Lab id="compost-lab" />

<ConceptCard title="Nitrogen fixers" emoji="🌱">
Plants need **nitrogen** to make proteins. Air is 78% nitrogen, but plants can't use it directly. **Rhizobium** bacteria in the **root nodules** of pulses (moong, chana, peas, beans) turn nitrogen from the air into compounds plants can use: **nitrogen fixation**. That's why rotating crops with pulses keeps soil fertile.
</ConceptCard>
