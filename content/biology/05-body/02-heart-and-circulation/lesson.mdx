<Hook emoji="🫀">
Make a fist: that's roughly the size of your heart. It beats about 100,000 times a day and around 2.5 billion times in a lifetime, never taking a break. Every minute it pushes your entire blood supply around your body. Where does the blood go, and what does it carry?
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="How many times does blood pass through the heart on one complete trip around the body?"
  options={["Once", "Twice", "Four times", "It doesn't pass through the heart"]}
  answer={1}
>
**Twice**: once to be pumped to the **lungs** to collect oxygen, and once to be pumped to the **body**. This is called **double circulation**.
</PredictFirst>

<Stage kind="explain" title="The circulatory system" />

<ConceptCard title="The heart" emoji="❤️">
The heart has **four chambers**: two **atria** (top) that receive blood and two **ventricles** (bottom) that pump it out. The **right side** pumps blood low in oxygen to the lungs; the **left side** pumps oxygen-rich blood to the body. **Valves** stop blood flowing backwards: the "lub-dub" of your heartbeat is the sound of valves closing.
</ConceptCard>

<MythFact myth="Blood in veins is blue.">
Blood is always red: bright red when full of oxygen and darker red when it has less. Veins only **look** blue through the skin because of how skin scatters light.
</MythFact>
