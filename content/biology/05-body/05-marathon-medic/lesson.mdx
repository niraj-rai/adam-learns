<Hook emoji="🏅">
Thousands of runners take part in the Bengaluru half marathon each year. For two hours, every system in their bodies works flat out: lungs, heart, blood, muscles, skin and kidneys, all coordinated perfectly. You're the medic following one runner. Can you read the body's signals?
</Hook>

<ConceptCard title="Your toolkit" emoji="🧰">
- **Muscles** need more oxygen and glucose to respire faster.
- **Breathing** speeds up: more oxygen in, more CO₂ out.
- **Heart rate** rises: faster delivery by the blood.
- **Skin** sweats: evaporation cools the body.
- **Kidneys** save water: less, darker urine.
- After exercise, extra oxygen helps muscles **recover**.
</ConceptCard>
