<PredictFirst

  answer={1}

Muscles can only **pull** (contract). That's why they work in **pairs**: one pulls a bone one way, and its partner pulls it back.
</PredictFirst>

<Stage kind="explain" title="The skeleton" />

<ConceptCard title="Jobs of the skeleton" emoji="🦴">
- **Support** and **shape**
- **Protection**: the skull protects the brain; the ribcage protects the heart and lungs.
- **Movement**, with muscles
- **Making blood cells** in the bone marrow

Many of a baby's bones **fuse** together as they grow, which is why adults have fewer.
</ConceptCard>
