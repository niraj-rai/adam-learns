<Lab id="food-tests" />

<ConceptCard title="Three tests" emoji="🧪">
- **Starch**: add dilute iodine → **blue-black** if present.
- **Protein**: add copper sulphate solution and sodium hydroxide → **violet** if present.
- **Fat**: rub the food on paper → an **oily, translucent patch** that lets light through.
</ConceptCard>
