<ConceptCard title="Special ways of feeding" emoji="🔍">
- **Parasitic**: takes food from a living **host**, harming it. Amarbel (*Cuscuta*), lice, tapeworms.
- **Insectivorous**: green plants that also trap insects. Pitcher plant, Venus flytrap.
- **Saprotrophic**: feed on dead and decaying matter. Mushrooms, bread mould, many bacteria.
- **Symbiotic**: two organisms live together and both benefit. **Lichens** (an alga + a fungus), and *Rhizobium* in pulse roots.
</ConceptCard>
