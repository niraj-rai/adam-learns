<Hook emoji="🩺">
Doctors and nutritionists are detectives too. From symptoms like bleeding gums, tiredness or a swollen neck, they work out which nutrient is missing, and often the cure is simply the right food. Your clinic opens now, with six patients waiting.
</Hook>

<ConceptCard title="Your toolkit" emoji="🧰">
- **Vitamin C** (amla, guava, citrus) → healthy gums and skin; lack causes **scurvy**.
- **Vitamin D + calcium** (sunlight, milk, ragi) → strong bones; lack causes **rickets**.
- **Vitamin A** (carrots, papaya, greens) → vision in dim light; lack causes **night blindness**.
- **Iron** (greens, jaggery, pulses, dates) → haemoglobin; lack causes **anaemia**.
- **Iodine** (iodised salt) → thyroid; lack causes **goitre**.
- **Protein** (dal, milk, eggs) → growth; lack causes poor growth (**kwashiorkor**).
</ConceptCard>

<RealLife title="India fights hidden hunger">
Programmes like **PM POSHAN** (the school midday meal scheme), iodised salt, and iron and folic acid supplements for teenagers aim to reduce deficiencies across India. Good nutrition in childhood helps people grow, learn and stay healthy for life.
</RealLife>
