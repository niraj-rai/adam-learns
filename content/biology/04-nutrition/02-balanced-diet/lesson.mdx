<Stage kind="explain" title="Balance" />

<ConceptCard title="A balanced diet" emoji="⚖️">
A <KeyTerm def="A diet containing all the nutrients, roughage and water in the right amounts for the body's needs.">balanced diet</KeyTerm> includes:
- **cereals and millets** (energy)
- **pulses, eggs, fish or meat, nuts** (protein)
- **vegetables and fruits** (vitamins, minerals, fibre)
- **milk products** (protein, calcium)
- only a **little** fat, sugar and salt.
</ConceptCard>

<DidYouKnow>
At India's suggestion, the United Nations declared 2023 the **International Year of Millets**. Millets like ragi, jowar and bajra are packed with fibre and minerals (ragi is rich in calcium) and need far less water to grow than rice.
</DidYouKnow>
