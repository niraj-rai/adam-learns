<Stage kind="explain" title="New cells from old" />

<ConceptCard title="Cell division" emoji="🔬">
New cells are made only when an existing cell **divides into two**. First the nucleus copies its DNA, then the cell splits. Cell division lets living things:
- **grow** (more cells),
- **repair** damage (healing a cut or a broken bone),
- **reproduce**: unicellular organisms like amoeba and bacteria split in two (**binary fission**).
</ConceptCard>

<Lab id="cell-division" />

<RealLife title="Food safety">
Bacteria in warm, moist food can divide every 20–30 minutes. That's why cooked food should be eaten fresh or cooled and refrigerated quickly: cold slows cell division right down, and cooking thoroughly kills many bacteria.
</RealLife>
