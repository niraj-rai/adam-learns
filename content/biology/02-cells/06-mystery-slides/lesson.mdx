<Hook emoji="🧪">
A box of microscope slides has arrived at the school lab, but the labels have fallen off! Some are plant cells, some animal cells, some bacteria. The lab technician needs your help to identify every one before tomorrow's class.
</Hook>

<ConceptCard title="Your toolkit" emoji="🧰">
- Start on **low power**, focus, then go higher. Bacteria need the highest power.
- **Cell wall + regular shape** → plant cells. **Green chloroplasts** → leaf cells.
- **No wall, irregular shape, one nucleus** → animal cells like cheek cells.
- **Tiny rods or dots, no nucleus** → bacteria.
- **Small round discs, no nucleus** → red blood cells.
- **Long branching extensions** → nerve cells.
</ConceptCard>

<CbseCorner source="Class 8 Curiosity Ch 2">
Exam questions often show a labelled or unlabelled diagram and ask whether it's a plant or animal cell, with reasons. Always give **two** features as evidence (e.g. cell wall and chloroplasts).
