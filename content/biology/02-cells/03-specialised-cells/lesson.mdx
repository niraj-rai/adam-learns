<PredictFirst

Without a nucleus, a red blood cell has **more room for haemoglobin**, the red substance that carries oxygen. Its dimpled disc shape also helps it take in oxygen and squeeze through narrow blood vessels.
</PredictFirst>

<ConceptCard title="Why cells are small" emoji="📦">
Everything a cell needs (oxygen, food, water) enters through its **surface**, but it must feed its whole **volume**. When a cell doubles in width, its surface area becomes 4 times bigger, but its volume becomes 8 times bigger. The surface can't keep up! So large organisms are built from many small cells, not one giant one.
</ConceptCard>
