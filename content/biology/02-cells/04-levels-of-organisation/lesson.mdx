<Hook emoji="🪜">
A cricket team isn't just eleven players standing around: batters, bowlers and a wicketkeeper each have roles, and together they make a team. Your body works in a similar way: cells team up into tissues, tissues into organs, organs into systems, and systems into you!
</Hook>

<Stage kind="explain" title="Five levels" />

<StepReveal>
<div>
**1. Cell**: the basic unit of life, e.g. a muscle cell.
</div>
<div>
**2. Tissue**: a group of similar cells doing the same job, e.g. muscle tissue.
</div>
<div>
**3. Organ**: different tissues working together, e.g. the stomach or a leaf.
</div>
<div>
**4. Organ system**: organs working together, e.g. the digestive system or a plant's shoot system.
</div>
<div>
**5. Organism**: a whole living thing, e.g. you, or a mango tree.
</div>
</StepReveal>

<RealLife title="Why doctors think in levels">
When you feel unwell, doctors think at every level: a blood test looks at **cells**, a biopsy at **tissues**, an X-ray or scan at **organs**, and a check-up considers whole **systems**, like your breathing or circulation.
</RealLife>
