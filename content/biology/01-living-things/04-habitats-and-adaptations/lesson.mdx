The snow leopard lives high in Ladakh, where winter nights drop far below freezing. The camel crosses the Thar Desert, where days are scorching. The lion-tailed macaque leaps through the rainforest canopy of the Western Ghats. Each looks perfectly designed for its home. How?

<PredictFirst
  question="Why does a camel have wide, padded feet?"
  options={["To run faster on roads", "So it doesn't sink into soft sand", "To swim", "To climb trees"]}
  answer={1}
>
Wide feet spread the camel's weight over a bigger area (remember **pressure = force ÷ area**!), so it doesn't sink into sand. Snow leopards' wide, furry paws work like snowshoes for the same reason.
</PredictFirst>

<Stage kind="explain" title="Habitats and adaptations" />

<ConceptCard title="Adaptations" emoji="🧬">
A <KeyTerm def="The place where an organism lives, which provides food, water, shelter and space.">habitat</KeyTerm> is where an organism lives. An <KeyTerm def="A feature of an organism's body or behaviour that helps it survive and reproduce in its habitat.">adaptation</KeyTerm> is a feature that helps it survive there. Adaptations develop over **many generations**, not in one animal's lifetime.
</ConceptCard>
