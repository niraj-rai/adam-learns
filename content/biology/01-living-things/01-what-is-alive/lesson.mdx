<Hook emoji="🤖">
A robot vacuum cleaner roams around the house, senses walls and even "feeds" itself at its charging dock. A seed sits in a jar for a year without doing anything at all. Which one is alive? It's trickier than it looks!
</Hook>

<PredictFirst

  answer={1}
>
The **seed**! It is **dormant**: resting, but alive, respiring very slowly. Give it water and warmth and it grows into a plant. The robot, the fire and the cloud each show one or two signs of life, but not all of them.
</PredictFirst>

<Stage kind="explain" title="The seven life processes" />

<ConceptCard title="MRS GREN" emoji="🌿">
Every living thing carries out **all seven** life processes:
- **M**ovement: moving itself or its parts (even plants turn towards light)
- **R**espiration: releasing energy from food
- **S**ensitivity: responding to changes around it
- **G**rowth: getting bigger by building new body parts
- **R**eproduction: making more of its own kind
- **E**xcretion: getting rid of wastes made in the body
- **N**utrition: taking in or making food
</ConceptCard>

<Stage kind="try" title="Is It Alive?" />

<Lab id="alive-lab" />

<MythFact myth="Plants are not alive because they can't move.">
Plants move, just slowly. Sunflowers track the Sun, roots grow towards water, and the **touch-me-not** (mimosa) folds its leaves in seconds when touched. That's sensitivity and movement!
</MythFact>

<DidYouKnow>
Some seeds stay alive for a very long time. Scientists in Israel grew a date palm from a seed about 2000 years old, found in the ruins of an ancient fortress!
</DidYouKnow>

<CbseCorner source="Class 6 Curiosity Ch 10: Living Creatures: Exploring their Characteristics">
- Living things show movement, respiration, growth, reproduction, response to stimuli, excretion and need for food.
- Non-living things may show some, but not all, of these characteristics.
- Seeds can remain dormant and still be alive.
</CbseCorner>
