<Hook emoji="🔍">
You find an unusual insect in the school garden. There are over a million named insect species, and no one can memorise them all. So how do scientists quickly find out what they're looking at? They follow a path of simple yes/no questions: a **key**.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="Which is the BEST question to use in a key for animals?"
  options={["Is it pretty?", "Does it have six legs?", "Is it big?", "Do I like it?"]}
  answer={1}
>
"Does it have six legs?" is about a **clear, observable feature** that anyone would answer the same way. "Pretty" and "big" depend on opinion.
</PredictFirst>

<Stage kind="explain" title="Dichotomous keys" />

<ConceptCard title="How keys work" emoji="🔑">
A <KeyTerm def="A tool for identifying organisms by answering a series of questions, each with two choices, that split the group in two.">dichotomous key</KeyTerm> ("dichotomous" means "cut in two") splits a group into two at every step using yes/no questions about features. Follow the answers and you end at a name.
</ConceptCard>

<StepReveal>
<div>
**Example key:** 1. Does it have a backbone? **Yes** → go to 2. **No** → go to 3.
</div>
<div>
2. Does it have feathers? **Yes** → sparrow. **No** → squirrel.
</div>
<div>
3. Does it have six legs? **Yes** → ant. **No** → spider.
</div>
</StepReveal>

<Stage kind="try" title="Key Builder" />

<Lab id="key-builder" />

<ConceptCard title="Tips for a good key" emoji="✅">
- Use features you can **see or check**: legs, wings, shell, fur, feathers.
- Avoid opinions (pretty, scary) and vague sizes (big, small).
- Colour can be unreliable: males, females and young can differ.
- A good question splits the group into two roughly **equal** parts, so you need fewer questions.
</ConceptCard>

<Stage kind="apply" title="Design your own" />

<Inquiry criterion="B" title="Make a leaf key">
Collect six different leaves. Design a dichotomous key that identifies each one. Swap with a friend and test each other's keys. Improve any question that caused confusion.
</Inquiry>

</Inquiry>
