<Stage kind="explain" title="Kingdoms and classes" />

<ConceptCard title="The five kingdoms" emoji="🌍">
- **Animals**: many cells; eat other organisms; most can move.
- **Plants**: many cells; make their own food by photosynthesis.
- **Fungi**: mushrooms, moulds, yeast; absorb food from dead or living matter.
- **Protists**: mostly single cells with a nucleus, like amoeba.
- **Bacteria**: tiny single cells with no nucleus, like the *Lactobacillus* in curd.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="Scientific names" emoji="🏷️">
Every species has a two-part **binomial name**, written in italics: first the **genus**, then the **species**. The tiger is *Panthera tigris*, the lion *Panthera leo* (the same genus, so closely related), and the mango *Mangifera indica*. Scientists everywhere use these names, whatever language they speak.
</ConceptCard>
