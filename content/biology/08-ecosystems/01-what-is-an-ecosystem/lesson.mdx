<Stage kind="explain" title="The parts of an ecosystem" />

<ConceptCard title="Biotic and abiotic">
**Biotic** components are the living parts: plants, animals, fungi and microbes. **Abiotic** components are the non-living parts: <KeyTerm def="The energy source for almost every ecosystem">sunlight</KeyTerm>, water, air, temperature, soil and minerals.
</ConceptCard>

<Lab id="ecosystem-builder" />

<MythFact myth="Decomposers are just 'rot' and aren't important.">
Without decomposers, dead leaves and bodies would pile up forever, and nutrients like nitrogen would stay locked inside them. Plants would run out of nutrients, and the whole ecosystem would starve.
</MythFact>

<DidYouKnow>
Ecosystems come in every size: a rotting log, a tide pool, a paddy field, the Sundarbans mangroves, or the whole Western Ghats.
</DidYouKnow>
