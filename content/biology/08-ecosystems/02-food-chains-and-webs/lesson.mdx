<Stage kind="explain" title="Chains and webs" />

<ConceptCard title="Food chain">
A <KeyTerm def="A sequence showing who eats whom, starting with a producer">food chain</KeyTerm> always starts with a **producer**. Each step is a **trophic level**: producer, then herbivore (primary consumer), then carnivore (secondary consumer), then top predator.
</ConceptCard>

<Formula tex="\text{Grass} \rightarrow \text{Grasshopper} \rightarrow \text{Frog} \rightarrow \text{Snake} \rightarrow \text{Eagle}" caption="A five-link food chain" />

<ConceptCard title="Food web">
Real animals eat many things. A <KeyTerm def="A network of interconnected food chains">food web</KeyTerm> shows all the feeding links. Because most animals have several food sources, webs are more **stable** than single chains.
</ConceptCard>
