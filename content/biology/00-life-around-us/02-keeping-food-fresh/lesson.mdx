<Stage kind="wonder" title="Why food goes bad" />

<Hook emoji="🥒">
Leave a slice of bread in a warm, damp place for a few days and it grows green fuzz. That fuzz is **mould**, a tiny living thing feeding on your bread. Tiny living things called **microbes** make food spoil.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="Which food will stay fresh longest without a fridge?"
  options={["Cooked rice", "Mango pickle", "Fresh milk", "Cut fruit"]}
  answer={1}
>
The salt, oil and spices in pickles stop microbes growing.
</PredictFirst>

<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="What microbes need" emoji="🦠">
Microbes grow fast when it is **warm**, **moist** and there is **food**. Take one of these away and food lasts longer.
</ConceptCard>
