<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="The 3 Rs" emoji="♻️">
**Reduce**: use less, e.g. carry a cloth bag. **Reuse**: use things again, e.g. refill bottles. **Recycle**: send paper, glass, metal and some plastics to be made into new things.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="Wet and dry waste" emoji="🗑️">
**Wet waste** (food, peels, leaves) can become compost for plants. **Dry waste** (plastic, paper, glass, metal) can be recycled. Keeping them separate makes both possible.
</ConceptCard>
