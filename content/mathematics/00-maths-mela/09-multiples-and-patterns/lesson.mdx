<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="Multiples" emoji="🐸">
Multiples of 4: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20… (4 × 1, 4 × 2, …). They go on forever. A **common multiple** of two numbers is a multiple of both: 12 and 24 are common multiples of 3 and 4.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="Patterns" emoji="🔢">
In 5, 9, 13, 17, … each number is **4 more** than the one before. Find the rule, and you can predict any number in the pattern.
</ConceptCard>
