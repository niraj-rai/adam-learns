<Hook emoji="🦋">
Fold a rangoli design, a butterfly or the Taj Mahal’s front in half and the two halves match exactly. That fold line is a **line of symmetry**.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="How many lines of symmetry does a square have?"
  options={["2", "4", "1", "0"]}
  answer={1}
>
Two through the middles of opposite sides and two along the diagonals.
</PredictFirst>

<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="Lines of symmetry" emoji="🦋">
A shape has a **line of symmetry** if folding along it makes the two halves match exactly. Square: 4. Rectangle: 2 (not the diagonals!). Equilateral triangle: 3. Circle: endless.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="Tiling" emoji="🧩">
Squares, rectangles, triangles and hexagons can **tile** a floor with no gaps. Circles leave gaps. Grandmother’s patchwork quilt uses repeating square patterns.
</ConceptCard>
