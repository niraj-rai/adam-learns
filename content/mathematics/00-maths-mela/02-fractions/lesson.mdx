<Stage kind="wonder" title="Fair shares" />

<Hook emoji="🍕">
Four friends share a pizza equally. Each gets **one quarter** (1/4). If three friends share another pizza, each gets 1/3. Who gets more: a friend in the group of four, or in the group of three?
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="Which is bigger: 1/3 or 1/4 of the same pizza?"
  options={["1/4", "1/3", "They are equal", "It depends on the topping"]}
  answer={1}
>
Sharing among fewer people gives bigger pieces.
</PredictFirst>

<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="Top and bottom" emoji="🍕">
In 3/4, the **denominator** (4) tells how many equal parts the whole is cut into. The **numerator** (3) tells how many parts we take.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="Equivalent fractions" emoji="🟰">
1/2 = 2/4 = 3/6 = 4/8. Multiply (or divide) the top and bottom by the same number and the fraction stays the same size.
</ConceptCard>

<StepReveal
  steps={[
    "Find 3/4 of 20 laddoos.",
    "1/4 of 20 = 20 ÷ 4 = 5.",
    "3/4 of 20 = 3 × 5 = 15 laddoos."
  ]}
/>
