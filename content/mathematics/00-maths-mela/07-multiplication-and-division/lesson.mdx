<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="Arrays" emoji="🌴">
An **array** is objects in equal rows. Rows × objects in each row = total. 3 × 5 = 5 × 3: turn the array and the total stays the same.
</ConceptCard>

<StepReveal
  steps={[

  ]}

<ConceptCard title="Division and remainders" emoji="🧺">
Share 29 coconuts into 4 sacks: each gets 7 (4 × 7 = 28), and **1 is left over**. We write 29 ÷ 4 = 7 remainder 1. The remainder is always less than the number you divide by.
</ConceptCard>
