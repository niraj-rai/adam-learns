<Hook emoji="🧭">
A dancer spins in a full circle. A door opens a quarter of the way. The minute hand of a clock sweeps round once every hour. All of these are **turns**, and the amount of turn is an **angle**.
</Hook>

<ConceptCard title="Naming angles" emoji="📐">
**Acute**: less than a right angle. **Right**: exactly 90°. **Obtuse**: between 90° and 180°. The size of an angle is about the turn, **not** the length of its arms.
</ConceptCard>
