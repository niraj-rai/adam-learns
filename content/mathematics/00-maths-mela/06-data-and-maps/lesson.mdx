<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="Tally marks" emoji="✏️">
Count with tally marks in groups of five: four lines and a fifth line across them. It makes counting quick.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="Graphs" emoji="📊">
A **pictograph** uses pictures; always read the **key** (1 picture = how many?). A **bar graph** uses bars that start at zero; the taller the bar, the bigger the number.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="Maps" emoji="🗺️">
Most maps have **North at the top**. A map uses a scale, like 1 cm = 1 km, and symbols explained in a legend.
</ConceptCard>
