The Indian Railways carries over 2 crore passengers every day! To read numbers that big, we need **place value**: each place is worth ten times the place to its right.
</Hook>

<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="Places" emoji="🔢">
Ones → Tens → Hundreds → Thousands → Ten thousands → **Lakhs** → Ten lakhs → **Crores**. In the Indian system we put commas after the hundreds and then after every two digits: **3,45,672**. In the international system we group in threes: **345,672**.
</ConceptCard>
