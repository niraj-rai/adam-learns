<MythFact myth="If it's a graph, it must be accurate.">
Graphs can mislead with **cut axes**, uneven scales, 3D effects that distort slices, or missing labels. Always check the axis, the scale and the source.
</MythFact>

<Stage kind="apply" title="Apply" />

<Inquiry criterion="D" title="Graph detective">
Find a chart in a newspaper, advert or website. Describe what it shows, check the axes and scale, and decide whether it could mislead. Redraw it honestly if needed, and explain your changes.
</Inquiry>
