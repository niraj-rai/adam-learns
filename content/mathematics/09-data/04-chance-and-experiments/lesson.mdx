<Hook emoji="🎲">
Toss a coin 10 times and you might get 7 heads. Does that mean the coin is biased? Toss it 10,000 times and you'll get very close to 5,000 heads. **Probability** is the maths of chance: it can't predict one toss, but it's amazingly accurate over many.
</Hook>
