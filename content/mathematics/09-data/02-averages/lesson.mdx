<Hook emoji="⚖️">
A village has nine families earning about ₹20,000 a month, and one very rich family earning ₹10,00,000. A newspaper says “average income: over ₹1,00,000!” Is that fair? The answer depends on **which** average you use: mean, median or mode.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="Which average is least affected by one extremely large value?"
  options={["Mean", "Median", "Both change the same", "Neither is affected"]}
  answer={1}
>
The **median** is just the middle value, so one huge value barely moves it. The **mean** adds everything up, so it gets dragged towards the outlier.
</PredictFirst>
