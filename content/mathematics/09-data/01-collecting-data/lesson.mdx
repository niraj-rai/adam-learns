<Hook emoji="📋">
Here are the heights of 40 students: 148, 156, 151, 162, 143, 155, … Can you tell what a typical height is, or how spread out they are, just by staring at the list? Neither can anyone else. **Organising** data into tables and pictures is how raw numbers turn into information.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="Heights are grouped 145–150, 150–155, 155–160. Where does 150 cm go?"
  options={["145–150", "150–155", "Both", "Neither"]}
  answer={1}
>
By convention, each class includes its **lower** limit but not its upper one. So 150 cm goes in **150–155**, and every value is counted exactly once.
</PredictFirst>
