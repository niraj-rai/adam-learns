<Hook emoji="🪜">
2³ = 8, 2² = 4, 2¹ = 2. Each step down, the exponent drops by 1 and the value halves. What happens if we keep going? What is 2⁰? What could 2⁻¹ possibly mean? Mathematicians didn't just invent answers; they let the **pattern** decide.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="Following the pattern 8, 4, 2, …, what is 2⁰?"
  options={["0", "1", "2", "It has no value"]}
  answer={1}
>
Halve 2 and you get **1**. So 2⁰ = 1. And the law of exponents agrees: 2³ ÷ 2³ = 2⁰, and anything divided by itself is 1.
</PredictFirst>

<MythFact myth="2⁻³ = −8.">
The minus sign in the exponent means **“one over”**, not “negative”. 2⁻³ = 1/2³ = 1/8. It's a small positive number.
</MythFact>
