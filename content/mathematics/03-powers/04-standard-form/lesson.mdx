<Hook emoji="🔭">
The Sun is 149,600,000,000 m away. A virus is 0.0000001 m long. The observable universe is about 880,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000 m across. Counting all those zeros is slow and easy to get wrong. Scientists write these numbers in **standard form**: 1.496 × 10¹¹ m, 1 × 10⁻⁷ m, 8.8 × 10²⁶ m.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="Which is 3,500,000 in standard form?"
  options={["35 × 10⁵", "3.5 × 10⁶", "3.5 × 10⁵", "0.35 × 10⁷"]}
  answer={1}
>
3,500,000 = 3.5 × 1,000,000 = **3.5 × 10⁶**. The number in front must be at least 1 and less than 10. The others have the right value but aren't in standard form (except 3.5 × 10⁵, which is wrong!).
</PredictFirst>
