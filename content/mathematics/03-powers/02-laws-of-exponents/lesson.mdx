<Hook emoji="✖️">
2³ × 2⁴: you could work out 8 × 16 = 128. Or you could notice that it's three 2s times four 2s: seven 2s, 2⁷ = 128. The **laws of exponents** are shortcuts, but they aren't magic. Every one of them comes from counting factors.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="What is a³ × a⁴?"
  options={["a⁷", "a¹²", "2a⁷", "a³⁴"]}
  answer={0}
>
(a × a × a) × (a × a × a × a) has **seven** a's, so a³ × a⁴ = a⁷. Add the exponents; don't multiply them.
</PredictFirst>

<MythFact myth="2³ × 3² = 6⁵ because you multiply the bases and add the exponents.">
The laws only work when the **bases are the same** (or, for (ab)ⁿ = aⁿbⁿ, when the exponents are the same). 2³ × 3² = 8 × 9 = 72, but 6⁵ = 7776.
</MythFact>
