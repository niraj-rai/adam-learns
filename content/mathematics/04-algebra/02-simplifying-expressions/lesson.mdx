<Hook emoji="🧩">
A shopkeeper counts: 3 boxes of pens, 2 loose pens, then 1 box sold, then 5 loose pens arrive. You don't need to open the boxes to describe what's left: 2 boxes and 7 loose pens. In algebra: 3x + 2 − x + 5 = **2x + 7**. Simplifying is just tidying up **like terms**.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="What is 4a + 3b − a + 2b?"
  options={["8ab", "3a + 5b", "5a + 5b", "3a + b"]}
  answer={1}
>
Collect the a's (4a − a = 3a) and the b's (3b + 2b = 5b): **3a + 5b**. a's and b's are unlike terms, so they stay separate.
</PredictFirst>

<ConceptCard title="What counts as ‘like’?">
<KeyTerm def="Terms with exactly the same letters raised to the same powers, like 3x² and −5x²">Like terms</KeyTerm> have the same letters with the same powers: 3x and −7x are like terms, but 3x and 3x² are not, and neither are 2xy and 2x.
</ConceptCard>

<MythFact myth="3x + 2 can be simplified to 5x.">
3x and 2 are **unlike terms**. Test with x = 10: 3x + 2 = 32, but 5x = 50. Different values, so they aren't equal.
</MythFact>
