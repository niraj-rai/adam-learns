<Hook emoji="🟦">
How would you work out 23 × 47 without a calculator? One way: split 23 into 20 + 3 and 47 into 40 + 7, and draw a rectangle. The four smaller rectangles are 800, 140, 120 and 21, and they add to 1081. The same idea, called the **distributive property**, lets us multiply expressions like (x + 3)(x + 2).
</Hook>

The 3 multiplies **everything** inside the bracket: 3 × x + 3 × 4 = **3x + 12**.

<MythFact myth="(x + 2)(x + 3) = x² + 6.">
That misses the two middle rectangles, 3x and 2x. The full answer is x² + 5x + 6. Test with x = 1: 3 × 4 = 12, and 1 + 5 + 6 = 12 ✓, but 1 + 6 = 7 ✗.
</MythFact>
