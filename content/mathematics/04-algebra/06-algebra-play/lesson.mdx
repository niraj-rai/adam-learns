<Stage kind="explain" title="Proving with algebra" />

<ConceptCard title="Examples vs proof">
Trying 3, 7 and 20 shows a trick works for those numbers. Using a letter x shows it works for **every** number: that's a **proof**. Mathematicians love proofs because they remove all doubt.
</ConceptCard>
