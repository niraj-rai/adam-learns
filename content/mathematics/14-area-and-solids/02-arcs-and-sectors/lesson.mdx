<Hook emoji="🍕">
Cut a pizza into 8 equal slices and each slice is 1/8 of the whole: 1/8 of the crust and 1/8 of the area. Each slice has a 45° angle at the centre, and 45/360 = 1/8. Every sector works this way.
</Hook>

90/360 = **1/4** of the circle: a quadrant.

<Stage kind="explain" title="Fraction of the whole" />

<Formula tex="\text{arc length} = \frac{\theta}{360} \times 2\pi r" />

<Formula tex="\text{sector area} = \frac{\theta}{360} \times \pi r^2" />

<ConceptCard title="Sectors and segments" emoji="🧩">
A <KeyTerm def="The region between two radii and the arc joining them, like a slice of pizza.">sector</KeyTerm> is bounded by two radii and an arc. A <KeyTerm def="The region between a chord and the arc it cuts off.">segment</KeyTerm> is bounded by a chord and an arc: segment area = sector area − triangle area. The perimeter of a sector = arc + 2r.
</ConceptCard>
