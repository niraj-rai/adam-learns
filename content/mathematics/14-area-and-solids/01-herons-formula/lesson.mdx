<Stage kind="explain" title="Heron's formula" />

<Formula tex="s = \frac{a + b + c}{2}" caption="The semi-perimeter" />

<Formula tex="\text{Area} = \sqrt{s(s - a)(s - b)(s - c)}" />

<ConceptCard title="Brahmagupta's formula" emoji="🔲">
In 628 CE Brahmagupta gave the area of a **cyclic** quadrilateral (all corners on a circle) with sides a, b, c, d: Area = √[(s − a)(s − b)(s − c)(s − d)], with s = (a + b + c + d)/2. Set d = 0 and you get Heron's formula!
</ConceptCard>
