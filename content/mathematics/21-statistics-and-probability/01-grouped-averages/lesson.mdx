<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="Formulas" emoji="📊">
**Mean** = Σfᵢxᵢ ÷ Σfᵢ (xᵢ = class mark). **Median** = l + ((n/2 − cf)/f) × h, where l is the lower limit of the median class, cf the cumulative frequency before it, f its frequency, h its width. **Mode** = l + ((f₁ − f₀)/(2f₁ − f₀ − f₂)) × h, using the modal class.
</ConceptCard>

<StepReveal
  steps={[
    "Heights: 140–145 (5), 145–150 (15), 150–155 (25), 155–160 (30), 160–165 (15), 165–170 (10). n = 100.",

    "Median = 155 + ((50 − 45)/30) × 5 = 155 + 0.83 ≈ 155.8 cm.",
    "Modal class 155–160: mode = 155 + ((30 − 25)/(60 − 25 − 15)) × 5 = 155 + 1.25 = 156.25 cm."
