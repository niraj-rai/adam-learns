<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="Number of zeros" emoji="📉">
A polynomial of degree n has **at most** n zeros. A quadratic graph (a parabola) can cross the x-axis twice, touch it once, or miss it.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="Zeros and coefficients" emoji="🔗">
If α and β are the zeros of ax² + bx + c, then **α + β = −b/a** and **αβ = c/a**. So a quadratic with zeros α and β is k[x² − (α + β)x + αβ].
</ConceptCard>
