<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="The theorem" emoji="🌳">
Every composite number can be written as a product of primes, and this factorisation is **unique**, apart from the order of the factors.
</ConceptCard>

<StepReveal
  steps={[

    "LCM = product of the greatest powers of all primes = 2⁵ × 3 × 101 = 9696.",
    "Check: HCF × LCM = 4 × 9696 = 38784 = 96 × 404."

<ConceptCard title="Proving √3 is irrational" emoji="🧠">
Suppose √3 = a/b in lowest terms. Then 3b² = a², so 3 divides a², and (because of unique factorisation) 3 divides a. Write a = 3c: then b² = 3c², so 3 divides b too. But a/b was in lowest terms, a contradiction. So √3 is irrational.
</ConceptCard>
