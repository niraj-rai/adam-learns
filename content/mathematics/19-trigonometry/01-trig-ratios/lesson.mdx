<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="SOH CAH TOA" emoji="📐">
For an acute angle θ in a right triangle: **sin θ = opposite/hypotenuse**, **cos θ = adjacent/hypotenuse**, **tan θ = opposite/adjacent = sin θ/cos θ**. The reciprocals are cosec θ, sec θ and cot θ.
</ConceptCard>
