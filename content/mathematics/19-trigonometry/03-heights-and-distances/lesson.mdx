<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="Elevation and depression" emoji="👀">
The **angle of elevation** is between the horizontal and the line of sight **up** to an object. The **angle of depression** is between the horizontal and the line of sight **down**. Between two points they are equal (alternate angles).
</ConceptCard>
