<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="Two triangles to remember" emoji="📐">
Half a square (sides 1, 1, √2) gives sin 45° = cos 45° = 1/√2 and tan 45° = 1. Half an equilateral triangle (sides 1, √3, 2) gives sin 30° = cos 60° = ½, sin 60° = cos 30° = √3/2, tan 30° = 1/√3 and tan 60° = √3.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="Identities" emoji="🟰">
From Pythagoras: **sin²θ + cos²θ = 1**. Divide by cos²θ: **1 + tan²θ = sec²θ**. Divide by sin²θ: **1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ**. Complementary angles: sin(90° − θ) = cos θ and tan(90° − θ) = cot θ.
</ConceptCard>
