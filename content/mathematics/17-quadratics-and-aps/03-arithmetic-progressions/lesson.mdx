<ConceptCard title="Formulas" emoji="🪜">
In an AP with first term a and common difference d: **aₙ = a + (n − 1)d** and **Sₙ = n/2 [2a + (n − 1)d] = n/2 (a + l)**, where l is the last term.
</ConceptCard>
