<Hook emoji="📦">
You want to wrap a gift box perfectly, with no overlaps and no gaps. How much paper do you need? Or paint a room: how many litres of paint for the walls? Both questions ask for **surface area**: the total area of all the faces of a solid.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="A cube has side 3 cm. What is its surface area?"
  options={["27 cm²", "54 cm²", "9 cm²", "36 cm²"]}
  answer={1}
>
A cube has 6 faces, each 3 × 3 = 9 cm², so its surface area is 6 × 9 = **54 cm²**. (27 cm³ is its volume.)
</PredictFirst>

<MythFact myth="If you double the size of a box, you need double the wrapping paper.">
Doubling every length multiplies the surface area by **4** (2 × 2), because area is two-dimensional. Try it in the lab.
</MythFact>
