<Hook emoji="🧊">
Unfold a cardboard box carefully and lay it flat: that flat shape is its **net**. A cube's net always has 6 squares, but not every set of 6 joined squares will fold into a cube. How many different cube nets are there? Mathematicians have proved the answer is exactly **11**.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="Will a 2 × 3 rectangle of squares fold into a cube?"
  options={["Yes", "No, two squares overlap", "Only if you cut it", "Only for large cubes"]}
  answer={1}
>
Try folding it in your head: two of the squares end up on the same face, leaving one face open. A 2 × 3 block is **not** a cube net.
</PredictFirst>
