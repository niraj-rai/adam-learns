<Hook emoji="⭕">
Wrap a string around a bangle, a plate or a bicycle wheel, then compare it with the distance across. Every single time, the string is just over **3 times** as long. That ratio, 3.14159265…, is called **π** (pi), and its digits go on forever without repeating.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="A wheel is 70 cm across. Roughly how far does it roll in one turn?"
  options={["70 cm", "140 cm", "About 220 cm", "About 490 cm"]}
  answer={2}
>
One turn rolls out the circumference: π × 70 ≈ 3.14 × 70 ≈ **220 cm**. That's why bigger wheels go further per turn.
</PredictFirst>
