<Hook emoji="✂️">
You know that a rectangle's area is length × width. Here's a secret: with a pair of scissors, you can turn a parallelogram, a triangle and even a trapezium into rectangles. That means you never need to memorise their formulas blindly: you can **see** where they come from.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="A parallelogram has base 6 and height 4. Its slanted side is 5. What is its area?"
  options={["30", "24", "20", "12"]}
  answer={1}
>
Cut a triangle off one end and slide it to the other: you get a 6 × 4 rectangle. Area = **24**. The slanted side (5) doesn't matter; only the **perpendicular height** does.
</PredictFirst>

<MythFact myth="The height of a triangle is the length of one of its sloping sides.">
The height is always the **perpendicular** distance from the base to the opposite corner, the straight-down distance at 90°. For an obtuse triangle, it can even fall outside the shape!
</MythFact>
