<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="Formulas" emoji="🍕">
Sector area = (θ/360) × πr². Arc length = (θ/360) × 2πr. **Minor segment = sector − triangle**. **Major segment = circle − minor segment**.
</ConceptCard>

  steps={[
