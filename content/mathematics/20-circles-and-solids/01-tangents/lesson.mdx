<Hook emoji="⭕">
When a bicycle chain leaves a gear, or a stone flies off a spinning sling, it moves along a **tangent**: a straight line that touches the circle at just one point.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="How many tangents can be drawn to a circle from a point outside it?"
  options={["One", "Two", "Three", "Infinitely many"]}
  answer={1}
>
Exactly two, one on each side.
</PredictFirst>

<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="Two theorems" emoji="⭕">
**1.** The tangent at any point of a circle is **perpendicular to the radius** through the point of contact. **2.** The lengths of the two tangents from an **external point** are **equal**.
</ConceptCard>

<StepReveal
  steps={[
    "Proof of theorem 2: tangents PA and PB from P to a circle with centre O.",
    "OA = OB (radii), OP is common, and angle OAP = angle OBP = 90° (theorem 1).",
    "So △OAP ≅ △OBP (RHS).",
    "Therefore PA = PB."
  ]}
