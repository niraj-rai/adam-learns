<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="Similar triangles" emoji="🔺">
Two triangles are **similar** if their corresponding angles are equal and their corresponding sides are in the same ratio (the scale factor). Criteria: **AA** (two angles equal), **SSS** (three sides in proportion), **SAS** (one equal angle between proportional sides).
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="Basic Proportionality Theorem" emoji="✂️">
If a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle, cutting the other two sides, it divides them in the same ratio: AD/DB = AE/EC. The converse is also true.
</ConceptCard>
