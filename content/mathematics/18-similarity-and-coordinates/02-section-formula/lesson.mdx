<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="The section formula" emoji="✂️">
The point P dividing A(x₁, y₁) to B(x₂, y₂) internally in the ratio m : n is
**P = ((m x₂ + n x₁)/(m + n), (m y₂ + n y₁)/(m + n))**. Notice m goes with B: P is nearer to A when m is smaller.
</ConceptCard>

<StepReveal
  steps={[
    "Café one third of the way from A(1, 2) to B(10, 8): the ratio AP : PB is 1 : 2.",
    "x = (1 × 10 + 2 × 1)/3 = 12/3 = 4.",
    "y = (1 × 8 + 2 × 2)/3 = 12/3 = 4.",
    "The café is at (4, 4)."
  ]}
/>

<ConceptCard title="Finding the ratio" emoji="🔎">
To find the ratio in which (−1, 6) divides (−3, 10) to (6, −8), write it as k : 1. Then (6k − 3)/(k + 1) = −1, so k = 2/7. The ratio is 2 : 7.
</ConceptCard>
