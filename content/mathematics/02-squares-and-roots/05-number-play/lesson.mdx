<MythFact myth="If a number is divisible by 2 and by 4, it must be divisible by 8.">
12 is divisible by 2 and 4, but not 8. The test for 8 needs the **last three digits**. Combined tests only work for co-prime pairs: 2 and 3 give 6, 3 and 4 give 12.
</MythFact>
