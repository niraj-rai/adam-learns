<ConceptCard title="What is a square number?">
A <KeyTerm def="A number you get by multiplying a whole number by itself, like 7 × 7 = 49">square number</KeyTerm> (or perfect square) is n × n = n². You can arrange that many dots in a perfect square: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, …
</ConceptCard>
