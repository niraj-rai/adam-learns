<Hook emoji="🎯">
A square garden has an area of 1225 m². How long is each side? Squaring turns a side into an area; a **square root** goes backwards, from area to side. For 1225 it's a neat 35 m. But what about a square with an area of 2 m²? Its side is a number whose decimals go on forever without repeating.
</Hook>

√64 asks “which number times itself makes 64?” 8 × 8 = 64, so √64 = 8. It is **not** 64 ÷ 2.

<DidYouKnow>
The **Śulba Sūtras**, ancient Indian texts on building fire altars (written roughly 800–500 BCE), give √2 ≈ 1 + 1/3 + 1/(3·4) − 1/(3·4·34) = 1.4142156…, correct to five decimal places.
</DidYouKnow>

<MythFact myth="√(9 + 16) = √9 + √16 = 3 + 4 = 7.">
√(9 + 16) = √25 = **5**, not 7. Square roots split over multiplication (√(4 × 9) = 2 × 3), but **not** over addition.
</MythFact>
