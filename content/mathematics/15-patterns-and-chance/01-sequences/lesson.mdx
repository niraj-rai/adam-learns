<ConceptCard title="Recursive and explicit" emoji="🔁">
A <KeyTerm def="A rule that gives each term from the one before, e.g. aₙ = aₙ₋₁ + 3.">recursive rule</KeyTerm> says how to get the next term from the previous one. An <KeyTerm def="A formula that gives any term directly from its position n, e.g. aₙ = 3n + 1.">explicit rule</KeyTerm> gives any term directly from n. Explicit rules let you predict far ahead.
</ConceptCard>
