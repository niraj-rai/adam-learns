<Hook emoji="🎲">
Will it rain tomorrow? Will your team win the toss? Will a new medicine work? We can't know for sure, but probability lets us measure **how likely** things are, and make better decisions under uncertainty.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="A fair coin lands heads 5 times in a row. What is the probability the next toss is heads?"
  options={["Less than 1/2: tails is 'due'", "More than 1/2: heads is on a streak", "Impossible to say", "1/2"]}
  answer={3}
>
The coin has no memory: each toss is **independent**, so P(heads) is still **1/2**.
</PredictFirst>
