<ConceptCard title="Every point on the line" emoji="📏">
A <KeyTerm def="An equation of the form ax + by + c = 0, where a and b are not both zero. Its graph is a straight line.">linear equation in two variables</KeyTerm>, ax + by = c, has infinitely many solutions: all the points on its line. Two such equations together usually have **one** common solution: where the lines cross.
</ConceptCard>

<Stage kind="try" title="Where Lines Cross" />

<Lab id="line-crossing" />

<ConceptCard title="One, none or infinitely many" emoji="🔀">
- Lines cross once → **one solution** (consistent).
- Lines parallel → **no solution** (inconsistent), e.g. x + y = 3 and x + y = 5.
- Same line → **infinitely many** (dependent), e.g. x + 2y = 4 and 2x + 4y = 8.
</ConceptCard>
