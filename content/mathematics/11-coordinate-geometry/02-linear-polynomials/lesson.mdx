<Formula tex="m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}" caption="Slope from two points: rise ÷ run" />

<ConceptCard title="Linear polynomials" emoji="🧮">
A <KeyTerm def="A polynomial of degree 1: p(x) = mx + c with m ≠ 0. Its graph is a straight line.">linear polynomial</KeyTerm> is p(x) = mx + c. Its <KeyTerm def="A value of x that makes the polynomial equal to zero; for mx + c it is x = −c/m.">zero</KeyTerm> is the value of x where p(x) = 0, the point where its graph crosses the x-axis: x = −c/m.
</ConceptCard>
