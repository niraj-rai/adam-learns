<Hook emoji="📍">
Your phone's map knows exactly where you are as two numbers: latitude and longitude. In the 1600s René Descartes had the same idea for geometry: describe every point with a pair of numbers (x, y). Suddenly shapes could be studied with algebra, and algebra could be drawn.
</Hook>

The gaps are 3 across and 4 up. By Pythagoras, the distance is √(3² + 4²) = √25 = **5**.

<ConceptCard title="The four quadrants" emoji="🧭">
The axes split the plane into four <KeyTerm def="One of the four regions of the Cartesian plane made by the x- and y-axes, numbered I to IV anticlockwise from the top right.">quadrants</KeyTerm>: I (+, +), II (−, +), III (−, −), IV (+, −). The x-coordinate is also called the **abscissa** and the y-coordinate the **ordinate**.
</ConceptCard>
