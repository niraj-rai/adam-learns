<Hook emoji="👷">
6 workers paint a school wall in 10 days. If 15 workers help, it should take fewer days. But how many? More workers means less time: when one quantity goes up, the other goes down. That's **inverse proportion**.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="6 workers take 10 days. How long for 12 workers?"
  options={["20 days", "5 days", "4 days", "16 days"]}
  answer={1}
>
Double the workers, half the time: **5 days**. The total work, 6 × 10 = 60 worker-days, stays the same: 12 × 5 = 60.
</PredictFirst>

<MythFact myth="Any time one quantity goes up while another goes down, it's inverse proportion.">
The **product** must stay constant. As a phone battery is used, time goes up and charge goes down, but time × charge isn't constant, so it isn't inverse proportion.
</MythFact>
