<Hook emoji="🏦">
Put ₹10,000 in a bank at 10% a year. With **simple interest**, you earn ₹1,000 every year. With **compound interest**, you earn interest on your interest too. After 25 years, simple interest gives ₹35,000, but compound interest gives about ₹1,08,000! Einstein is often (probably wrongly) quoted as calling compound interest the eighth wonder of the world.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="₹10,000 at 10% compound interest for 2 years becomes…"
  options={["₹12,000", "₹12,100", "₹11,000", "₹20,000"]}

Year 1: ₹10,000 × 1.1 = ₹11,000. Year 2: ₹11,000 × 1.1 = **₹12,100**. The extra ₹100 is interest earned on the first year's interest.
</PredictFirst>

<DidYouKnow>
The **rule of 72**: divide 72 by the interest rate to estimate how many years it takes money to double. At 6%, about 12 years; at 9%, about 8 years.
</DidYouKnow>
