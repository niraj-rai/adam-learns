<Hook emoji="☕">
Amma's masala chai recipe makes 4 cups. Suddenly 6 relatives arrive. How much milk, sugar and tea leaves do you need now? If you double the cups, you double everything; for 6 cups, you multiply everything by 6/4 = 1.5. That's **direct proportion**.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="4 cups of chai need 8 tsp of sugar. How much sugar for 6 cups?"
  options={["10 tsp", "12 tsp", "14 tsp", "24 tsp"]}
  answer={1}
>
Unitary method: 8 ÷ 4 = 2 tsp per cup, then 2 × 6 = **12 tsp**. Adding 2 to both (6 cups, 10 tsp) would make the chai less sweet.
</PredictFirst>

<DidYouKnow>
The **rule of three** (*trairāśika*) was described by Āryabhaṭa in 499 CE and used by Indian merchants for centuries. It travelled to Europe, where it was once called “the golden rule” of arithmetic.
