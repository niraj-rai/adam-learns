<Hook emoji="🏪">
You buy cricket bats for ₹1500 each and label them ₹2000. A customer asks for a discount. How much can you give before you start losing money? And once GST is added, what does the customer actually pay? Every shopkeeper needs this maths.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="You bought a bat for ₹1500 and sell it for ₹1800. What is your profit percentage?"
  options={["300%", "20%", "16.7%", "30%"]}
  answer={1}
>
Profit = ₹300. Profit % is always calculated on the **cost price**: 300 ÷ 1500 × 100 = **20%**.
</PredictFirst>
