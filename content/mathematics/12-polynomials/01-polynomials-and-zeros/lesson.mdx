<Stage kind="explain" title="The language of polynomials" />

<ConceptCard title="What counts as a polynomial?" emoji="🧮">
A <KeyTerm def="An expression made of terms of the form a·xⁿ, where n is a whole number (0, 1, 2, …) and a is a number.">polynomial</KeyTerm> has terms like 5x³, −2x and 7, with whole-number powers. 1/x and √x are **not** allowed. The <KeyTerm def="The highest power of the variable in a polynomial with a non-zero coefficient.">degree</KeyTerm> is the highest power: linear (1), quadratic (2), cubic (3).
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="Zeros" emoji="0️⃣">
A <KeyTerm def="A value of x for which p(x) = 0; on the graph, where the curve crosses the x-axis.">zero</KeyTerm> of p(x) is a value where p(x) = 0. A polynomial of degree n has **at most** n zeros.
</ConceptCard>
