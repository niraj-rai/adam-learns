<DidYouKnow>
The word **algebra** comes from the Arabic *al-jabr*, meaning “restoring”, from the title of a book by al-Khwarizmi (about 820 CE). *Al-jabr* meant moving a subtracted term to the other side so it becomes added: exactly what we do when solving equations.
</DidYouKnow>
