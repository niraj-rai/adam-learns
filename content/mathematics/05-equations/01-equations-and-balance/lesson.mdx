<Hook emoji="⚖️">
On one pan of a balance: 3 identical sealed bags and 2 one-kilogram weights. On the other: 11 one-kilogram weights. The scale is perfectly level. How heavy is one bag? You can work it out without opening anything, just by keeping the balance level.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="3 bags + 2 kg balance 11 kg. How heavy is one bag?"
  options={["2 kg", "3 kg", "4 kg", "11/3 kg"]}
  answer={1}
>
Take 2 kg off **both** pans: 3 bags balance 9 kg. Split both pans into 3 equal groups: 1 bag balances 3 kg. In algebra: 3x + 2 = 11, so 3x = 9 and **x = 3**.
</PredictFirst>

<Stage kind="explain" title="Keep it balanced" />

<ConceptCard title="What is an equation?">
An <KeyTerm def="A statement that two expressions are equal, like 3x + 2 = 11">equation</KeyTerm> says two things have the same value. Solving it means finding the value of the unknown that makes it true, the <KeyTerm def="The value of the unknown that makes an equation true">solution</KeyTerm>. The golden rule: **whatever you do to one side, do to the other.**
</ConceptCard>

<Lab id="balance-scale" />

<MythFact myth="You just ‘move a number to the other side and change its sign’.">
That shortcut works, but only because you are really **subtracting (or adding) the same amount on both sides**. Understanding why stops you making sign mistakes.
</MythFact>
