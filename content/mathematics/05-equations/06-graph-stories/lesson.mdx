<Hook emoji="📊">
A line climbs steadily, goes flat, then climbs more steeply. Without a single word, it tells you that someone walked, stopped at a shop, then hurried to school. Reading graphs like this is a superpower in science, sport, news and business.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="On a graph of water in a tank against time, what does a line sloping downwards mean?"
  options={["The tank is filling", "The tank is draining", "The tank is full", "Time is going backwards"]}
  answer={1}

As time goes on, there's less water: the tank is **draining**. The steeper the slope, the faster it drains.
</PredictFirst>

<MythFact myth="A distance–time graph shows the shape of the path, like hills.">
A graph of distance against time is **not a picture of the route**. A line going up means moving further away, even on flat ground.
</MythFact>
