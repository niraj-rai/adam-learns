An auto-rickshaw charges ₹30 to start plus ₹15 per km. Plot the fare for 0, 1, 2, 3… km and the points line up perfectly. With that straight line, you can predict the fare for any distance, or find how far ₹120 will take you. That's the power of a **linear graph**.
