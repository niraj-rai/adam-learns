<Hook emoji="🧭">
Around 300 BCE, Euclid of Alexandria wrote the *Elements*, which became one of the most-studied books in history. He began with 23 definitions and a handful of statements so obvious that everyone would accept them, and from those proved hundreds of results. Centuries earlier, Indian priests were already using ropes and pegs in the **Śulba Sūtras** to construct precise altars.
</Hook>

<DidYouKnow>
Euclid's fifth postulate (about parallel lines) puzzled mathematicians for 2 000 years. In the 1800s they discovered that changing it gives completely new, consistent geometries, which turned out to describe curved space in Einstein's theory of gravity.
</DidYouKnow>
