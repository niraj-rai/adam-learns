<Hook emoji="🧱">
You know that the angles of a triangle add to 180°. But **why**? Measuring a thousand triangles only shows it's true for those thousand. A proof shows it must be true for **every** triangle, forever.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="Two triangles have all three angles equal. Must they be congruent?"

  answer={1}
>
AAA only guarantees the same **shape** (similar triangles), not the same size. Congruence needs at least one pair of equal sides.
</PredictFirst>
