<MythFact myth="The sign rules are just rules to memorise.">
They are the only rules that keep maths **consistent**. If (−1) × (−1) were −1, the distributive law would break: (−1) × (1 + (−1)) must be 0, but −1 + (−1) would give −2!
</MythFact>

<Inquiry criterion="B" title="Counting negatives">

</Inquiry>
