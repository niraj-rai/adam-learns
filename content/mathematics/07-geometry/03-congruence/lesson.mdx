<Stage kind="explain" title="The congruence tests" />

<ConceptCard title="Congruent">
Two shapes are <KeyTerm def="Exactly the same shape and size; one can be moved, turned or flipped to fit exactly on the other">congruent</KeyTerm> if one fits exactly on the other, even if you have to turn or flip it. Then all their <b>corresponding parts</b> are equal.
</ConceptCard>

<RealLife>
