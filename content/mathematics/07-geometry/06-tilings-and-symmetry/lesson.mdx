<Hook emoji="🐝">
Look at a bathroom floor, a honeycomb, or the jaali screens of the Taj Mahal. The shapes fit together perfectly, with no gaps and no overlaps. Why do bees choose hexagons? Why are floors square or hexagonal, but never made of regular pentagons?
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="Can regular pentagons tile a floor with no gaps?"
  options={["Yes", "No", "Only large ones", "Only if you alternate colours"]}
  answer={1}
>
Each angle of a regular pentagon is 108°. Three make 324° (a gap), four make 432° (an overlap). Only shapes whose angles divide **360°** exactly can meet at a point.
</PredictFirst>

<ConceptCard title="Symmetry">
A shape has <KeyTerm def="A mirror line that splits a shape into two matching halves">line symmetry</KeyTerm> if it can be folded onto itself, and <KeyTerm def="Looking the same after a turn of less than 360°; the order is how many times it fits in one full turn">rotational symmetry</KeyTerm> if it looks the same after a turn. A regular hexagon has 6 lines of symmetry and rotational symmetry of order 6.
</ConceptCard>
