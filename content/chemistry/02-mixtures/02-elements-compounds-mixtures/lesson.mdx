<Hook emoji="🧂">
**Sodium** is a soft metal that explodes in water. **Chlorine** is a poisonous green gas. Join them together chemically and you get… the **table salt** you sprinkle on your food. How can two dangerous things make something so safe?
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="When sodium and chlorine join to make salt, the salt…"
  options={["is still explosive and poisonous", "has completely new properties", "is half metal, half gas", "can be separated with a magnet"]}
  answer={1}
>
Salt has **completely new properties**. When elements **chemically join**, they form a **compound**: a brand-new substance that behaves differently from the elements it came from.
</PredictFirst>

<Stage kind="explain" title="The building blocks" />

<ConceptCard title="Element" emoji="🧱">
An **element** is a pure substance made of only **one kind of atom**. It cannot be broken down into anything simpler by chemical reactions.

There are **118 known elements**. Each has a **symbol**: H (hydrogen), O (oxygen), C (carbon), Fe (iron, from Latin *ferrum*), Au (gold, from Latin *aurum*).

Some elements exist as single atoms (like gold, Au) and some as small **molecules**, like oxygen gas, <Chem>O2</Chem>, where two oxygen atoms are joined.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="Compound" emoji="🔗">
A **compound** is a pure substance made of **two or more elements chemically joined** in a **fixed ratio**.

- Water, <Chem>H2O</Chem>: always 2 hydrogen atoms for every 1 oxygen atom.
- Carbon dioxide, <Chem>CO2</Chem>: always 1 carbon for every 2 oxygen.
- Table salt, <Chem>NaCl</Chem>: always 1 sodium for every 1 chlorine.

A compound has **new properties** and can only be split up by a **chemical reaction**.
</ConceptCard>

<Stage kind="see" title="Watch a mixture become a compound" />

<Lab id="iron-sulfur" />

<MythFact myth="A compound is just its elements mixed together.">
In a mixture, the particles are just **next to each other**. In a compound, atoms are **chemically bonded**, and that bond changes everything. Iron is magnetic and grey; sulfur is yellow. Iron **sulfide** is black and **not magnetic**. You can't pull the iron out with a magnet any more!
</MythFact>

<MythFact myth="If it's made of molecules, it must be a compound.">
Oxygen gas is made of <Chem>O2</Chem> **molecules**, but both atoms are oxygen, so it's an **element**. A molecule is a compound only if it contains **different** kinds of atoms.
</MythFact>

<DidYouKnow>
Just **four elements** (oxygen, carbon, hydrogen and nitrogen) make up about **96% of your body's mass**. Most of it is in compounds like water, proteins and fats.
</DidYouKnow>
