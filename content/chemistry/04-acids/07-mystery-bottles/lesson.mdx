<Hook emoji="🏷️">
Disaster in the school lab: six bottles of colourless liquid have lost their labels. One is a strong acid, one a weak acid, one pure water, one salt water, one a weak base and one a strong base. They all look exactly the same. You have only **12 tests**. Can you save the day?
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="Which ONE test would give you the most information about all six bottles?"
  options={["Blue litmus", "Turmeric", "Universal indicator", "Magnesium ribbon"]}
  answer={2}
>
**Universal indicator** gives a pH value, not just "acid or base". It can separate strong from weak acids and strong from weak bases in one go. But two bottles will still look the same… which ones?
</PredictFirst>

<Stage kind="explain" title="Plan before you test" />

<ConceptCard title="Think like a scientist" emoji="🧠">
Good investigators **plan** before they act:

1. List what you're looking for, and **how each substance is different**.
2. Choose the test that splits the group **most** first.
3. Only use extra tests where substances still **look the same**.
4. Record every result clearly, and use evidence to justify each label.

Hint: pure water and salt water are both **pH 7**. What physical method from Unit 2 could tell them apart?
</ConceptCard>
