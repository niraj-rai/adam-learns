<Stage kind="explain" title="Atomic mass units" />

<ConceptCard title="The atomic mass unit" emoji="⚖️">
1 <KeyTerm def="The unit for the masses of atoms: exactly one-twelfth of the mass of one carbon-12 atom. Symbol u.">atomic mass unit (u)</KeyTerm> is one-twelfth of the mass of a carbon-12 atom. On this scale H = 1 u, C = 12 u, N = 14 u, O = 16 u, Na = 23 u, Cl = 35.5 u.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="Molecular mass" emoji="🧮">
The <KeyTerm def="The sum of the atomic masses of all the atoms in a molecule.">molecular mass</KeyTerm> is the sum of the atomic masses of all atoms in a molecule. For ionic compounds (which form giant lattices, not molecules) we call it the **formula unit mass**, calculated the same way.
- H₂O: 2 × 1 + 16 = 18 u
- H₂SO₄: 2 × 1 + 32 + 4 × 16 = 98 u
- Ca(OH)₂: 40 + 2 × (16 + 1) = 74 u
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="Percentage by mass" emoji="📊">
% of an element = (mass of that element in the formula ÷ molecular mass) × 100. In water, hydrogen is 2 ÷ 18 × 100 ≈ 11%, which matches the 1 : 8 mass ratio from the law of constant proportions.
</ConceptCard>
