<ConceptCard title="Steps in metallurgy" emoji="🏭">
1. **Concentrate** the ore (remove sand and rock, the gangue).
2. **Roasting** (sulfide ores heated in air) or **calcination** (carbonates heated without air) → oxide.
3. **Reduce** the oxide to the metal.
4. **Refine** it, e.g. by electrolytic refining: impure metal as the anode, pure metal deposits on the cathode.
</ConceptCard>
