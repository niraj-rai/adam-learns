<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="Naming salts" emoji="🏷️">
A salt's first name comes from the base's metal (or ammonium) and its second from the acid: sulfuric acid → **sulfates**, hydrochloric → **chlorides**, nitric → **nitrates**, carbonic → **carbonates**. Strong acid + strong base → neutral salt; strong acid + weak base → acidic; weak acid + strong base → basic.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="Water of crystallisation" emoji="💎">
Some salts trap a fixed number of water molecules in their crystals: <KeyTerm def="The fixed number of water molecules in one formula unit of a salt's crystal, e.g. CuSO₄·5H₂O.">water of crystallisation</KeyTerm>. Blue CuSO₄·5H₂O loses it on heating and turns white; gypsum is CaSO₄·2H₂O.
</ConceptCard>
