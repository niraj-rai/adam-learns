<Stage kind="wonder" title="What are clothes made of?" />

<Hook emoji="👕">
Look at the label on your school shirt. It might say “100% cotton” or “polyester and cotton”. Every piece of cloth starts as thin threads called **fibres**, from plants, animals or factories.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="Which fibre comes from an animal?"
  options={["Cotton", "Wool", "Jute", "Polyester"]}
  answer={1}
>
Wool comes from sheep, goats and yaks.
</PredictFirst>

<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="Fibre → yarn → fabric" emoji="🧵">
Fibres are **spun** (twisted) into yarn. Yarn is **woven** on a loom or **knitted** with needles to make **fabric**. Fabric is cut and stitched into clothes.
</ConceptCard>
