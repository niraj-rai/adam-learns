<Hook emoji="🍳">
Ice melts into water, and you can freeze it back. But once a roti is cooked, you can never turn it back into dough. Some changes can be undone; others can’t.
</Hook>

<ConceptCard title="New substances" emoji="✨">
In changes that can’t be undone, **new substances** are made. Burning wood makes ash, smoke and gases. Curd is different from milk. These are chemical changes.
</ConceptCard>
