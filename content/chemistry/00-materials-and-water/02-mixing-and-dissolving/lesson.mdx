<Stage kind="wonder" title="Where did the sugar go?" />

<Hook emoji="🥤">
Stir a spoon of sugar into water and it vanishes. But taste the water: it’s sweet! The sugar is still there, broken into pieces too tiny to see. It has **dissolved**.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="You stir sand into water. What happens?"
  options={["It dissolves", "It settles at the bottom", "It turns into sugar", "It floats forever"]}
  answer={1}
>
Sand is **insoluble**: it doesn’t dissolve.
</PredictFirst>

<ConceptCard title="Separating" emoji="🧺">
Let heavy bits **settle**, then pour off the water. **Filter** through cloth or paper to catch solid bits. **Evaporate** salty water in the sun to get salt back, as salt makers do along the coast of Gujarat.
</ConceptCard>
