<Hook emoji="🚰">
A glass of water can look clear and still carry germs that cause illness. Every year, millions of children fall sick from unsafe water. Knowing how to clean water saves lives.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="Which step kills germs in water?"
  options={["Letting mud settle", "Pouring off the top", "Boiling", "Filtering through cloth"]}
  answer={2}
>
Boiling kills most germs; settling and filtering remove dirt.
</PredictFirst>

<ConceptCard title="Where does water come from?" emoji="🏞️">
Rain fills rivers, lakes and ponds, and soaks into the ground to become groundwater in wells and borewells. Many cities pipe water from dams far away. Only a tiny part of Earth’s water is fresh water we can use.
</ConceptCard>
