<Stage kind="explain" title="Detective toolkit" />

<ConceptCard title="Clues you can use" emoji="🧰">
- **Protons / atomic number** → identifies the element directly.
- **Electron arrangement** → number of shells = **period**; outer electrons = **group**.
- **Family properties** → alkali metal? halogen? noble gas?
- **Metal or non-metal**, **state** at room temperature, and **everyday uses**.
</ConceptCard>

<Inquiry criterion="C" title="Create a riddle">
Write a **3-clue riddle** for an element from the first 20 that wasn't in the challenge (e.g. lithium, nitrogen, sulfur or potassium).

1. Clue 1 should be the **hardest** (a property or family).
2. Clue 2 should narrow it down (period, group or electron arrangement).
3. Clue 3 should make it certain (a fact or use).

Test your riddle on a friend or family member!
</Inquiry>
