<ConceptCard title="Why the opposite trends?" emoji="🔄">
- **Group 1 atoms LOSE an electron.** Bigger atoms hold their outer electron less tightly → easier to lose → **more reactive** down the group.
- **Group 17 atoms GAIN an electron.** Bigger atoms attract an extra electron less strongly → harder to gain → **less reactive** down the group.
</ConceptCard>

<MythFact myth="Noble gases can never react with anything.">
For a long time scientists thought so. But in 1962, the chemist Neil Bartlett made a compound of **xenon**. The heavier noble gases can react with very reactive elements like fluorine. Helium, neon and argon still haven't been made to form stable compounds in normal conditions.
</MythFact>
