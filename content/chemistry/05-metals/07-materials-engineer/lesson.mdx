<Inquiry criterion="D" title="Design brief: a school water bottle">
Your school wants to replace plastic bottles with reusable bottles for all 1,000 students.

1. Compare **stainless steel**, **aluminium**, **glass** and **hard plastic** using: safety, weight, durability, cost and environmental impact.
2. Which would you recommend? Justify using at least **three properties**.
3. What is one **disadvantage** of your choice, and how could it be reduced?
</Inquiry>

<CbseCorner source="Class 7 Curiosity Ch 4 · Class 10 Ch 3">
- NCERT questions often ask *why* a metal is used for a purpose: aluminium for foil and aircraft, copper for wires, iron and steel for construction, gold and silver for jewellery.
- Always answer with a **property** (malleable, ductile, conductor, light, unreactive, strong).
</CbseCorner>
