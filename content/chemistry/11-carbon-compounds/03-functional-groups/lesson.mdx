Ethane is a gas used as fuel. Replace one hydrogen with –OH and you get ethanol, a liquid used in sanitisers and blended into petrol. Replace it with –COOH and you get the sour acid in vinegar. Small groups, big differences.
</Hook>

<ConceptCard title="Esters" emoji="🍎">
A carboxylic acid and an alcohol, warmed with a little concentrated sulfuric acid, form an <KeyTerm def="A sweet-smelling compound made from an acid and an alcohol; esters give fruits and perfumes their scent.">ester</KeyTerm> and water: **esterification**. Heating an ester with NaOH gives back the alcohol and the sodium salt of the acid: this is **saponification**, how soap is made.
</ConceptCard>
