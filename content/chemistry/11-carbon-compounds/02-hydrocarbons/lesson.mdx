<ConceptCard title="Homologous series" emoji="📈">
A <KeyTerm def="A family of compounds with the same general formula and functional group, in which each member differs from the next by CH₂.">homologous series</KeyTerm> has the same general formula and similar chemical properties; each member has one more CH₂. Physical properties like boiling point change gradually along the series. Butane can also be branched: compounds with the same formula but different structures are <KeyTerm def="Compounds with the same molecular formula but different structures.">isomers</KeyTerm>.
</ConceptCard>
