<Hook emoji="🕵️">
An electrician arrives at a house where the lights won't work. There are dozens of possible causes: a tripped MCB, a loose connection, a blown bulb, a faulty switch. Good electricians don't guess. They check things in a logical order, like detectives following clues. Can you solve seven cases?
</Hook>

<ConceptCard title="Your detective toolkit" emoji="🧰">
- Current needs a **complete loop** of **conductors**.
- Cells must face the **same way**; a **dead** cell can't push.
- A **switch** must be closed; a **fused bulb** has a gap inside.
- An **LED** works only one way round (long leg to +) and needs about **2 V**.
- Change **one thing at a time**, just like a fair test.
</ConceptCard>
