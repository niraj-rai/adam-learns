<ConceptCard title="Staying safe" emoji="⚠️">
The 230 V supply in our homes can cause severe shocks, burns and even death, so never experiment with it.
- Water conducts because of dissolved salts, so **never touch switches or appliances with wet hands**.
- Don't use damaged or bare wires; don't overload sockets.
- Stay far away from fallen or overhead power lines, and never fly kites near them.
- Our experiments use only 1.5 V cells, which are safe.
</ConceptCard>
