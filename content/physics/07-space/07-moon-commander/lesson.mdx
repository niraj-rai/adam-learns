Now it's your turn: can you put the Moon exactly where it needs to be for each mission?
</Hook>

<ConceptCard title="Your toolkit" emoji="🧰">
- **New moon (Amavasya)**: the Moon is between Earth and the Sun. **Full moon (Purnima)**: Earth is between them.
- **First quarter**: a quarter of the way round, lit on the right (from India). **Last quarter**: lit on the left; it rises around midnight.
- The evening crescent is a **waxing** crescent.
- Eclipses need **new or full moon + the Moon at a node**.
</ConceptCard>
