<Hook emoji="📅">
Makar Sankranti is almost always on 14 or 15 January. Diwali moves between mid-October and mid-November. Eid comes about 11 days earlier each year, so over time it falls in every season. Why do different festivals follow such different patterns?
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="How many days are there in 12 lunar months (12 cycles of Moon phases)?"
  options={["365 days", "About 354 days", "360 days", "About 340 days"]}
  answer={1}
>
12 × 29.5 ≈ **354 days**, about **11 days shorter** than the solar year of 365¼ days. That mismatch is the key to the puzzle!
</PredictFirst>

<Stage kind="explain" title="Three clocks in the sky" />

<ConceptCard title="Day, month, year" emoji="⏳">
- **Day**: one rotation of Earth.
- **Month**: one cycle of Moon phases (about 29.5 days).
- **Year**: one revolution of Earth around the Sun (about 365¼ days). It sets the seasons.

These don't divide into each other neatly, so every calendar has to make a choice.
</ConceptCard>
