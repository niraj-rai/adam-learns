<Stage kind="explain" title="The Sun and its family" />

<ConceptCard title="Stars, planets and moons" emoji="⭐">
- A **star**, like the Sun, is a huge ball of hot gas that makes its own light and heat.
- A **planet** orbits a star and shines by reflected light. There are eight: four small rocky ones (Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars) and four giants (Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune).
- A **moon** (natural satellite) orbits a planet.
- Distances are measured in **astronomical units (AU)**: 1 AU is the Earth–Sun distance, about 150 million km. Sunlight takes about **8 minutes** to cross it.
</ConceptCard>
