<Hook emoji="🪞">

<PredictFirst
  question="You stand 1 m in front of a mirror. How far away does your image seem to be from YOU?"
  options={["1 m", "2 m", "0 m: it's on the mirror", "0.5 m"]}
  answer={1}

The image is **1 m behind** the mirror, so it is **2 m** from you. Your brain traces the reflected rays straight back to a point behind the glass.
</PredictFirst>

<Stage kind="explain" title="The image in a plane mirror" />

<ConceptCard title="Five facts about plane mirror images" emoji="🖐️">
1. **Virtual**: the light doesn't really come from behind the mirror, so the image can't be caught on a screen.
2. **Upright**: not upside down.
3. **Same size** as the object.
4. **As far behind** the mirror as the object is in front.
5. <KeyTerm def="The swapping of left and right in a mirror image.">Laterally inverted</KeyTerm>: left and right are swapped.
</ConceptCard>

<RealLife title="Periscopes and kaleidoscopes">
- A **periscope** uses two parallel mirrors at 45° to see over walls, crowds or, from a submarine, above the sea. Each mirror turns the light through 90°.
- A **kaleidoscope** has three mirrors joined in a triangle. Multiple reflections turn a few bits of coloured glass into endless symmetrical patterns, like a rangoli!
</RealLife>
