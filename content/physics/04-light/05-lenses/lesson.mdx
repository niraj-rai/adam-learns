</ConceptCard>

<Lab id="optics-bench" mode="lens" />

<MythFact myth="A magnifying glass makes the object bigger.">
The object stays the same size! The lens bends the light so that it **appears** to come from a bigger, virtual image further away.
</MythFact>

<RealLife title="Lenses and your eyes">
Your eye has a convex lens that focuses light onto the retina. In **short sight**, distant objects focus in front of the retina, and a **concave** lens corrects it. In **long sight**, close objects focus behind the retina, and a **convex** lens helps.
</RealLife>

<CbseCorner source="Class 8 Curiosity Ch 10">
- **Convex (converging)** and **concave (diverging)** lenses.
- A convex lens can form real, inverted images or virtual, magnified images; a concave lens forms virtual, erect, diminished images.
- Uses: magnifying glass, spectacles, cameras, microscopes and telescopes.
</CbseCorner>
