</Hook>

A **spectrum** of seven colours: **VIBGYOR** (violet, indigo, blue, green, yellow, orange, red). The colours were hidden inside white light all along!

<Stage kind="explain" title="Dispersion" />

<ConceptCard title="Splitting white light" emoji="🔺">
Each colour of light bends (refracts) by a slightly different amount in glass. **Violet bends the most, red the least.** So a prism spreads white light into a <KeyTerm def="The band of colours produced when white light is split up: violet, indigo, blue, green, yellow, orange and red.">spectrum</KeyTerm>. This is called **dispersion**.
</ConceptCard>

<Stage kind="try" title="Prism and Colour Lab" />

<Lab id="prism-lab" />

<ConceptCard title="How rainbows form" emoji="🌦️">
Each raindrop acts like a tiny prism: sunlight enters, is **dispersed**, reflects off the back of the drop and comes out spread into colours. Millions of drops together make a rainbow. You can only see one with the **Sun behind you** and rain in front.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="Mixing it back" emoji="🎡">
- A second, upside-down prism **recombines** the spectrum into white light.
- **Newton's disc**, painted with the seven colours, looks almost white when spun fast.
- A coloured **filter** doesn't add colour. It **absorbs** the other colours and lets only its own through.
</ConceptCard>

<DidYouKnow>
The physicist **C. V. Raman** won India's first Nobel Prize in science (1930) for discovering how light changes colour slightly when it scatters off molecules: the **Raman effect**. India celebrates **National Science Day** on 28 February, the day he announced it in 1928.
</DidYouKnow>
