<Hook emoji="🛠️">
Engineers solve problems with physics every day. A tractor is stuck, a knife won't cut, a scooter skids, a dam must hold back a river, and a cyclone is coming. Can you use everything you've learned about **forces, friction, gravity and pressure** to fix eight real problems?
</Hook>

<ConceptCard title="Your toolkit" emoji="🧰">
- **Net force**: balanced forces cause no change; unbalanced forces change motion.
- **Friction**: increase it for grip, reduce it to save energy.
- **Weight = mass × g**: mass stays the same; weight depends on gravity.
- **Pressure = force ÷ area**: a small area cuts; a large area spreads the load.
- **Liquid pressure** increases with depth; **air pressure** pushes on everything.
- **Winds** blow from high to low pressure; warm seas power cyclones.
</ConceptCard>
