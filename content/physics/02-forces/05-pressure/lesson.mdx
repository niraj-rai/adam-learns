<Stage kind="wonder" title="Heels vs elephants" />

<Hook emoji="🐘">
An elephant weighs about as much as 80 people. A person in stiletto heels weighs much less. Yet the heel can dent a wooden floor that the elephant's foot wouldn't! How can a lighter person do more damage?
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="The same person stands on soft snow in four ways. In which will they sink DEEPEST?"
  options={["Two feet", "One foot", "One stiletto heel", "Snowshoes"]}
  answer={2}
>
Their weight (the force) is the same each time. On a heel, the whole force is squeezed onto a **tiny area**, so the **pressure** is enormous and they sink right in.
</PredictFirst>

<Stage kind="explain" title="Force spread over an area" />

<Formula tex="\text{pressure} = \frac{\text{force}}{\text{area}}" caption="Units: N/cm², or N/m² = pascal (Pa)" />

<ConceptCard title="Two ways to change pressure" emoji="🎛️">
- **Bigger force** → bigger pressure. **Bigger area** → smaller pressure.
- To **cut or pierce**: make the area tiny (knives, needles, nail tips, drawing pins).
- To **avoid sinking or hurting**: make the area large (wide tyres, bag straps, camel feet, snowshoes).
</ConceptCard>

<Stage kind="try" title="Pressure Lab" />

<Lab id="pressure-lab" />

<StepReveal>
<div>
**Worked example:** a 450 N person stands on two feet with a total area of 300 cm². What is the pressure?
</div>
<div>
pressure = 450 ÷ 300 = **1.5 N/cm²**
</div>
<div>
On one stiletto heel (area ≈ 1 cm²): 450 ÷ 1 = **450 N/cm²**, which is 300 times more!
</div>
</StepReveal>

<QuickCheck question="Why is it easier to cut vegetables with a sharp knife than a blunt one?" options={["The sharp knife is heavier", "The sharp edge has a smaller area, so the same force gives more pressure", "The blunt knife has more friction", "Sharp knives are made of stronger steel"]} answer={1}>
Same push, smaller area → **greater pressure** → it cuts easily.
</QuickCheck>

<DidYouKnow>
Camels can walk on loose desert sand in Rajasthan without sinking because their broad, padded feet spread their weight over a large area. Their feet act like natural snowshoes!
</DidYouKnow>

<CbseCorner source="Class 8 Curiosity Ch 6">
- **Pressure = force ÷ area**; the SI unit is the **pascal (Pa) = N/m²**.
- Same force on a smaller area gives more pressure: sharp knives, pointed nails.
- Larger area gives less pressure: wide straps, broad tyres on heavy vehicles.
</CbseCorner>
