<Stage kind="explain" title="How the eye focuses" />

<ConceptCard title="Parts of the eye" emoji="👁️">
Light enters through the **cornea** (which does most of the bending), passes the **pupil** (its size controlled by the **iris**), and is focused by the **eye lens** onto the **retina**, where light-sensitive cells send signals along the optic nerve.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="Accommodation" emoji="🔄">
<KeyTerm def="The eye's ability to change the focal length of its lens, using the ciliary muscles, to focus on near and far objects.">Accommodation</KeyTerm>: the ciliary muscles make the lens thicker for near objects and thinner for far ones. A normal eye sees clearly from the **near point** (about 25 cm) to the **far point** (infinity).
</ConceptCard>
