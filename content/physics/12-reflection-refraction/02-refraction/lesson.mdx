<Hook emoji="🪙">
Put a coin in an empty cup and move back until it just disappears behind the rim. Now have a friend pour in water, and the coin comes back into view without moving. Light bends as it leaves the water: **refraction**.
</Hook>

<Stage kind="explain" title="The laws of refraction" />

<ConceptCard title="Two laws" emoji="📏">
1. The incident ray, the refracted ray and the normal all lie in the same plane.
2. <KeyTerm def="n₁ sin i = n₂ sin r: the ratio sin i / sin r is constant for a given pair of media and colour of light.">Snell's law</KeyTerm>: sin i / sin r is constant for a given pair of media.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="Total internal reflection" emoji="💡">
Going from a denser to a less dense medium, light bends away from the normal. At the <KeyTerm def="The angle of incidence in the denser medium for which the angle of refraction is 90°.">critical angle</KeyTerm> the refracted ray skims the surface; beyond it, all the light reflects back inside. Optical fibres, endoscopes and the sparkle of diamonds use this.
</ConceptCard>
