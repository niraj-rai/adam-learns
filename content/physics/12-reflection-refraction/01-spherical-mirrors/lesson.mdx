<Stage kind="explain" title="The mirror formula" />

<ConceptCard title="New Cartesian sign convention" emoji="➕➖">
- Measure all distances from the **pole** P.
- Distances in the direction of the incident light (usually to the right) are **positive**; against it, **negative**. So the object distance u is negative.
- Heights above the principal axis are positive, below negative.
- A concave mirror has a negative f; a convex mirror a positive f. And f = R/2.
</ConceptCard>

<Formula tex="\frac{1}{v} + \frac{1}{u} = \frac{1}{f} \qquad m = \frac{h'}{h} = -\frac{v}{u}" caption="v = image distance, u = object distance, f = focal length, m = magnification" />
