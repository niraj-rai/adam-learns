<Hook emoji="🔍">
The same convex lens can be a magnifying glass, a camera lens or a projector lens: it depends only on where the object is. And the number printed on a spectacle prescription, like "−1.5", is the lens's **power**.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="A convex lens has a focal length of 50 cm. What is its power?"
  options={["+50 D", "+2 D", "−2 D", "+0.02 D"]}
  answer={1}
>
Power P = 1/f with f in **metres**: 1 ÷ 0.5 = **+2 D** (dioptres).
</PredictFirst>
