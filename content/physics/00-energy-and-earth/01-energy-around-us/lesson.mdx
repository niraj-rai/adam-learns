<Hook emoji="⚡">
A pressure cooker whistles, a ceiling fan spins, a torch shines, and you run to catch the school bus. All of these need **energy**. Energy is what makes things move, light up, heat up and make sound.
</Hook>

<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="Energy changes form" emoji="🔄">
Energy is never lost; it **changes form**. In a torch, stored energy in the battery becomes electricity, then light. In your body, food energy becomes movement and heat.
</ConceptCard>
