<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="Revolution" emoji="🪐">
While spinning, the Earth also travels round the Sun in a path called an **orbit**. One trip takes about **365¼ days**: one year. Every four years the extra quarters add up to one day, giving a **leap year** with 29 February.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="The tilt" emoji="↗️">
The Earth’s axis is tilted. For part of the year, the northern half (where India is) leans towards the Sun: the Sun is high, days are long, and it is **summer**. Six months later it leans away: **winter**. India also has a **monsoon** season when rain-bearing winds arrive.
</ConceptCard>
