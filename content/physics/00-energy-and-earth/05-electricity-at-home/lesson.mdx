<Hook emoji="💡">
A torch needs a cell, a bulb, a switch and a path for electricity to flow all the way round. That complete path is called a **circuit**. Break it anywhere, and the light goes out.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="In a torch, what does the switch do?"
  options={["Makes electricity", "Opens or closes the circuit", "Stores light", "Cools the bulb"]}
  answer={1}
>
On closes the gap so current flows; off opens it.
</PredictFirst>

<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="A complete circuit" emoji="🔄">
Electricity flows from one end of the cell, through the wires and the bulb, and back to the other end. The path must be **complete** (closed) with no gaps.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="Stay safe" emoji="⚠️">
Mains electricity at home is very dangerous. Never touch switches with wet hands, never poke anything into sockets, and never fly kites near power lines.
</ConceptCard>
