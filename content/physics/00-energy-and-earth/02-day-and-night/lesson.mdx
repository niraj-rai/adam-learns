<Hook emoji="🌍">

</Hook>

<Stage kind="explain" title="Key ideas" />

<ConceptCard title="Rotation" emoji="🔄">
The Earth spins on an imaginary line called its **axis**. One spin takes about **24 hours**. The half facing the Sun has **day**; the half facing away has **night**.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="East to west" emoji="🌅">
The Earth spins from west to east. So we see the Sun **rise in the east** and **set in the west**. Arunachal Pradesh, in the far east of India, sees sunrise first.
</ConceptCard>
