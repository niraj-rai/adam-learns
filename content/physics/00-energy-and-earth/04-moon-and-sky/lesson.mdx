<ConceptCard title="Stars and patterns" emoji="✨">
Stars are faraway suns that make their own light. People have long joined bright stars into patterns like Saptarishi (the Big Dipper). As the Earth spins, the stars seem to move across the night sky.
</ConceptCard>
