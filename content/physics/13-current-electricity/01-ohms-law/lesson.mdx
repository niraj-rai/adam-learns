For a resistor at constant temperature, current is proportional to voltage: **double the voltage, double the current**.

<Lab id="ohms-law" />

<MythFact myth="Current gets used up as it goes round a circuit.">
The current is the **same** all the way round a series circuit. What gets transferred is **energy**: the cell gives energy to the charges, and they give it to the components.
</MythFact>
