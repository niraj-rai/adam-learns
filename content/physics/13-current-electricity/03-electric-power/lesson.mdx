<Hook emoji="🔥">
Electric irons, geysers, toasters and incandescent bulbs all turn electrical energy into heat on purpose. But the same heating effect in overloaded wiring can start fires. Understanding it helps us use electricity well and safely.
</Hook>

<Stage kind="try" title="Power, Heat and Fuses" />

<Lab id="fuse-box" />

<ConceptCard title="Staying safe" emoji="🛡️">
- **Overloading**: too many appliances on one circuit draw too much current.
- **Short circuit**: live and neutral touch, so resistance is almost zero and the current is huge.
- A **fuse** (a thin wire that melts) or an **MCB** (a switch that trips) cuts the supply. The **earth wire** protects you if a metal case becomes live.
</ConceptCard>
