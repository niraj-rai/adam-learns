<Hook emoji="🧭">
In 1820, during a lecture, Hans Christian Ørsted noticed a compass needle twitch every time he switched on a current in a nearby wire. That accident revealed that **electricity and magnetism are linked**, the idea behind every motor, speaker and MRI scanner.
</Hook>

<ConceptCard title="Field lines" emoji="🧲">
Magnetic <KeyTerm def="Lines that show the direction a north pole would be pushed; they run from N to S outside a magnet, never cross, and are closer where the field is stronger.">field lines</KeyTerm> go from N to S outside a magnet, never cross, and are closer together where the field is stronger.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="Right-hand thumb rule" emoji="👍">
Hold the wire in your right hand with the thumb pointing along the current: your curled fingers show the direction of the magnetic field.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="Solenoids and electromagnets" emoji="🌀">
A <KeyTerm def="A long coil of wire. When current flows, its magnetic field is like a bar magnet's, strong and uniform inside.">solenoid</KeyTerm> behaves like a bar magnet with a strong, uniform field inside. Put a soft iron core inside and you get an **electromagnet**: stronger with more turns and more current, and switched off simply by stopping the current. Scrap-yard cranes, electric bells and relays use them.
</ConceptCard>
