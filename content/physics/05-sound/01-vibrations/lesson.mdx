Gripping the bell **stops it vibrating**, so the sound stops. No vibration, no sound!

<Stage kind="explain" title="Vibrations make sound" />

<ConceptCard title="What is a vibration?" emoji="〰️">
A <KeyTerm def="A rapid to-and-fro (back-and-forth) movement of an object about a fixed position.">vibration</KeyTerm> is a quick to-and-fro movement. Every sound is produced by something vibrating: a string, a stretched skin, a column of air, or your **vocal cords** in the voice box (larynx).
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="Frequency" emoji="⏱️">
The number of vibrations per second is the <KeyTerm def="The number of complete vibrations per second, measured in hertz (Hz).">frequency</KeyTerm>, measured in **hertz (Hz)**. A mosquito's wings beat hundreds of times a second, which is why it has a high-pitched whine.
</ConceptCard>

<DidYouKnow>
In the tabla, the black circle on the drumhead (the **syahi**, made of a paste including iron filings) changes how the skin vibrates. It gives the tabla its clear, musical ring, a design perfected by Indian craftsmen centuries ago.
