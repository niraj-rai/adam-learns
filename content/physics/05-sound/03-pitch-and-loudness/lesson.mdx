<ConceptCard title="Beyond our hearing" emoji="🦇">
- Humans hear about **20 Hz to 20,000 Hz** (the upper limit falls as we get older).
- **Infrasound** is below 20 Hz: elephants and whales use it; earthquakes produce it.
- **Ultrasound** is above 20,000 Hz: bats, dolphins and dogs hear it. Doctors use it to scan babies before birth, and ships use it in SONAR.
</ConceptCard>

<MythFact myth="High-pitched sounds are always louder.">
Pitch and loudness are independent. A mosquito's whine is high but quiet; a truck's rumble is low but loud.
</MythFact>

<CbseCorner source="Earlier NCERT Class 8 Ch 13 and Class 9 Sound">
- **Frequency** decides **pitch**; **amplitude** decides **loudness**.
- The audible range for humans is about **20 Hz to 20,000 Hz**; infrasound and ultrasound lie outside it.
- Loudness is measured in **decibels (dB)**.
</CbseCorner>
