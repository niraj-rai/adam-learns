<ConceptCard title="Conservation of energy" emoji="♻️">
**Energy cannot be created or destroyed. It can only change from one form to another.** Devices are energy *changers*: a fan turns electrical energy into kinetic energy, and a solar panel turns light into electrical energy. At every step some energy spreads out as **heat**, which is hard to use again. We call this "wasted" energy.
</ConceptCard>

<QuickCheck question="In a hydroelectric power station, which order is correct?" options={["Electrical → kinetic → gravitational", "Gravitational potential → kinetic → electrical", "Chemical → thermal → electrical", "Light → electrical → kinetic"]} answer={1}>
Water stored high up (gravitational potential energy) falls (kinetic energy) and spins a turbine connected to a generator (electrical energy).
</QuickCheck>

<RealLife title="India's energy mix">
India makes electricity from coal, hydro (like the dams on the Kaveri and Bhakra Nangal), wind (Tamil Nadu and Gujarat), solar (the huge Bhadla Solar Park in Rajasthan) and nuclear power (Kudankulam, Tarapur). Each is a different energy chain ending in electrical energy.
</RealLife>
