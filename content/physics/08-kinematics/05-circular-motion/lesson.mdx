It flies off **along the tangent**, in a straight line in the direction it was moving at that instant. Nothing pushes it outwards; the string simply stops pulling it inwards.
</PredictFirst>

<Stage kind="explain" title="Constant speed, changing velocity" />

<ConceptCard title="Uniform circular motion">
An object moving in a circle at constant speed has a velocity that is always along the **tangent**, so its **direction** keeps changing. A changing velocity means an **acceleration**, directed towards the centre of the circle. A force towards the centre (tension, friction, or gravity) is needed to keep it turning.
</ConceptCard>

<RealLife>
