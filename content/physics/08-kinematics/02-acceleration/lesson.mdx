<Hook emoji="🚗">
Car adverts boast “0 to 100 km/h in 8 seconds!” A metro train glides smoothly to a stop at every station. A cricket ball changes from 140 km/h to 0 in a fielder's hands in a split second. All of these are about **acceleration**: how quickly velocity changes.
</Hook>

<PredictFirst
  question="A car's velocity goes 0, 3, 6, 9, 12 m/s at 1-second intervals. What is its acceleration?"
  options={["12 m/s²", "3 m/s²", "0 m/s²", "6 m/s²"]}
  answer={1}
>
It gains **3 m/s every second**, so the acceleration is 3 m/s² (metres per second, per second).
</PredictFirst>

<MythFact myth="A very fast object must be accelerating a lot.">
A plane cruising at 900 km/h in a straight line has **zero** acceleration. Acceleration is about *changing* velocity, not about being fast.
</MythFact>
