## L3: Grades 9–10

Tissues · Diversity of life · Life processes (nutrition, respiration, transport, excretion) · Control & coordination (nervous system, hormones) · Reproduction · Heredity & evolution (Mendel) · Ecology & human impact. Mapped to IB MYP 4–5 Biology and CBSE 9–10.

## L4: Grades 11–12

IB DP Biology themes (Unity & diversity, Form & function, Interaction & interdependence, Continuity & change), mapped to CBSE 11–12 (NEET base) when this level starts.
