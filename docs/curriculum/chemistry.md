# Chemistry Curriculum Map

> Built first. **Phase 1 scope = Levels L0–L2 (up to Grade 8)**, marked 🎯.
> L3 (Grades 9–10) and L4 (Grades 11–12) are mapped here so the content structure can grow without being reorganized.

**Legend**
- **IB**: MYP year (MYP 1 = Gr 6, MYP 2 = Gr 7, MYP 3 = Gr 8, MYP 4–5 = Gr 9–10), then DP (Gr 11–12)
- **CBSE**: NCERT class. "Cur-8 Ch7" = new *Curiosity* Class 8 textbook, chapter 7. "Old-8 Ch4" = pre-2025 NCERT Class 8 chapter, kept because many schools and reference books still use it.
- **★**: complexity 1–5 (see [00-master-plan.md](../00-master-plan.md#4-complexity-scale-used-on-every-topic))
- ⚠️ CBSE chapter numbers for the new *Curiosity* series have only been confirmed for Class 8 Ch 7 (Particulate Nature of Matter) and Ch 8 (Nature of Matter: Elements, Compounds and Mixtures). Check the others against the NCERT PDF before tagging.
- IB MYP 1–3 sciences are school-designed. The MYP years below are the *typical* placement. Reorder to match the school's unit plan.

## L0–L2: Foundations to Grade 8 🎯 (Phase 1)

### Unit 1: Matter and Its Nature ✅ built
*IB Key Concept:* **Systems** · *Related:* Form, Models · *Global Context:* Scientific & technical innovation
*Statement of Inquiry:* "Models of tiny particles help us explain the properties of everything around us."

| # | Topic | IB | CBSE | ★ | Interactive lab / experience | Practice highlights |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 🎯 | What is matter? (mass, volume, "takes up space") | MYP 1 | 6 | ★ | **Matter or Not?** swipe cards (air, light, sound, water, feelings) | Sort into bins |
| 1.2 🎯 | States of matter & the particle model | MYP 1–2 | Cur-8 Ch7, 9 Ch1 | ★★ | **Particle Simulator**: pick a substance, then a temperature slider shows solid/liquid/gas particle motion live | Predict & observe; match state to particle diagram |
| 1.3 🎯 | Changes of state: melting, freezing, boiling, evaporation, condensation, sublimation | MYP 2 | Cur-8 Ch7, 9 Ch1 | ★★★ | Particle Simulator + a **heating curve** plotted live, showing the plateau when heat energy breaks bonds | Label heating-curve regions; order steps |
| 1.4 🎯 | Diffusion & particle motion (agarbatti smell, ink in water) | MYP 2 | Cur-8 Ch7 | ★★ | **Diffusion Race**: hot vs cold water, gas vs liquid, particle size | Fair-test design (Crit. B) |
| 1.5 🎯 | Interparticle forces & spaces, compressibility | MYP 2–3 | Cur-8 Ch7 | ★★★ | **Syringe squeeze**: compress solid, liquid, and gas | Explain with particle model (short answer) |
| 1.6 | Density: why things float or sink | MYP 3 | 9 (Gravitation) | ★★★ | **Density Tower**: drop objects into liquid layers; calculate mass/volume | Numeric with units |

**Misconceptions to target:** particles expand when heated (it is the *gaps* that grow); there is air between particles; evaporation only happens at 100 °C; the bubbles in boiling water are air.

## L4: Grades 11–12 (Phase 5, on demand)

**IB DP Chemistry** (syllabus first assessed 2025), organized as *Structure* and *Reactivity*:

| DP theme | Contents | CBSE 11–12 equivalent | ★ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Structure 1: Models of the particulate nature of matter | Particulate nature, nuclear atom, electron configurations, mole, ideal gases | 11: Basic Concepts, Structure of Atom, States of Matter* | ★★★★★ |
| Structure 2: Models of bonding & structure | Ionic, covalent, metallic, VSEPR, hybridisation (HL), materials | 11: Chemical Bonding | ★★★★★ |
| Structure 3: Classification of matter | Periodic table trends, functional groups, nomenclature | 11: Classification of Elements; Organic Basics; 12: d & f block, Coordination Compounds | ★★★★★ |
| Reactivity 1: What drives chemical reactions? | Enthalpy, Hess's law, bond enthalpies, entropy and Gibbs energy (HL) | 11: Thermodynamics | ★★★★★ |
| Reactivity 2: How much, how fast, how far? | Stoichiometry, kinetics, equilibrium | 11: Equilibrium; 12: Chemical Kinetics, Solutions | ★★★★★ |
| Reactivity 3: Mechanisms of chemical change | Proton transfer (acid-base), electron transfer (redox, electrochemistry), electron sharing & electron-pair (organic mechanisms) | 11: Redox, Hydrocarbons; 12: Electrochemistry, Haloalkanes, Alcohols, Aldehydes, Amines, Biomolecules | ★★★★★ |

\* CBSE rationalized syllabi have moved or removed some chapters. Re-check the current NCERT list when Phase 5 starts.
